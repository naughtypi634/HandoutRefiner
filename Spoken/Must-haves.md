# Must-haves

**Things You Own**

- Name three things you have with you right now, off the top of your head.
- What was your most recent purchase? Was it worth it?
- What was your last impulse buy? Do you regret it?
- What was your last buyer's remorse? What happened?
- What is the one thing you never leave home without?
- What do you own but never use and still cannot throw away?
- What is your most expensive possession? How do you protect it?

**Tech and Apps**

- Which gadgets are must-haves for you: a laptop, earbuds, or a watch?
- Which apps could you not live without? Name three.
- How much did your phone cost? Was it worth the money?
- Do you buy the newest model every year, or keep yours for years?
- Do you buy things you see in short videos? What was the last one?
- Do you use a shopping app for groceries, clothes, or both?
- What device do you wish someone would invent?

**Home Essentials**

- What are must-haves for your living room?
- What are must-haves for your bedroom and bathroom?
- What are must-haves for the kitchen or the dining table?
- What is one home item you bought and now love?
- What is one home item that turned out to be a waste of money?
- Do you spend more on quality or on a cheaper price?
- How do you decide when to replace something at home?

**Travel and Fitness**

- What are must-haves in your travel bag?
- What do you always forget to pack? How did that go?
- Do you own fitness gear you rarely use? What is it?
- What exercise gear is actually worth buying?
- Do you bring your own pillow on trips? Why or why not?
- What is the best thing you ever bought for travel?
- Do you pack light, or take everything you might need?

**Money and Priorities**

- What are your top ten essentials? Rank them.
- What is the most important thing you own? Why?
- If you had to sell everything but one thing, what would you keep?
- What do you spend too much money on? Be honest.
- What do you refuse to spend money on, no matter what?
- What did you once think you needed but turned out not to?
- What would you buy first if you won a million yuan?

**Shopping Habits**

- Do you shop online or in stores? Which do you prefer?
- Do you compare prices before buying? How many apps do you check?
- Do you read reviews before buying? Do you trust them?
- Have you ever waited in line for a new product? What was it?
- Do you buy things because of ads or influencers? Give an example.
- What is the last thing you bought for someone else?
- Do you keep receipts and warranties, or throw them away?
