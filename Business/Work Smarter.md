# Work Smarter

**Calendar**

- Do you plan your day with the calendar app on your phone?
- What is the most efficient way to use a calendar?
- Do you fill every slot, or leave white space between tasks?
- How much empty time do you need between two meetings?
- Do you set reminders for the things you must not forget?
- Does your company make you use its own chat, file, or office app?
- Which app do you open first when you start your workday?

**WeChat at Work**

- Do you use WeChat for work? What frustrates you most about it?
- Do you use the floating window to switch between two chats?
- Do you quote or @ people so they actually see your message?
- Do you mark a chat unread so you come back to it later?
- How do you keep your personal WeChat away from your work chats?
- Have you ever sent a work message to the wrong person?
- What is one WeChat habit that wastes your time?

**Focus**

- Have you heard of the Pomodoro Technique? Do you use it?
- Can you work for 90 minutes straight, or do you need breaks?
- What usually breaks your focus — your phone, messages, or noise?
- How do you stop your phone from pulling you away from work?
- Do you check messages on a schedule, or whenever they arrive?
- What time of day is your most productive hour? Why?
- Do you write a to-do list for the day, or just start working?

**Tools**

- What about GTD, to-do lists, or the Eisenhower Matrix? Do you use them?
- Have you ever gone looking for a productivity app? Did it help?
- What is the one app that actually saves you time every day?
- Do you use a paper notebook, or only your phone? Why?
- Have you ever tried a tool and given up after a week?
- Do you keep a record of your work? What do you use?
- Would you pay for a productivity app if it really worked?

**Habits and Overwork**

- What do you think of "productivity obsession"?
- Is being busy the same as being productive? Why?
- Do you ever work on weekends, or do you keep them free?
- How do you stop a small task from eating a whole afternoon?
- Do you plan tomorrow's tasks the night before?
- What is one bad habit you want to break this month?

**What Works for You**

- What is your number-one rule for a productive day?
- Have your habits changed since you started working from home?
- What do you do when you feel you are getting nothing done?
- Who is the most organized person you know? What do they do?
- Is it possible to work smart and still finish on time?
- What advice would you give a new colleague about staying focused?
