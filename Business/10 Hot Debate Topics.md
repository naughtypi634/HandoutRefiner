# 10 Hot Debate Topics · 十个热门职场辩题

## AI Will Replace Most Office Jobs · AI 会取代大多数办公室工作

- Do you use AI at work now? What do you use it for?
- Has AI already taken over any tasks on your team?
- If AI writes the report in one minute, is it still your work?
- Should a company tell you when AI takes over part of your job?
- Which job is safer in ten years: a translator or a plumber?

## Return-to-Office Orders Are Right · 应该回办公室上班

- How many days a week are you in the office now?
- Is it fair for a company to order everyone back five days a week?
- Do you get more done at home or in the office? Give an example.
- What do you lose when you never meet your team in person?
- If you had to choose, would you take more money or more days at home?

## No Work Messages After 10 p.m. · 晚上十点后不该发工作消息

- Do you get WeChat messages from work after 10 p.m.?
- Do you answer them right away? Why or why not?
- Is "just one quick question" ever really quick?
- Should a boss be able to fine you for not replying?
- What is a polite way to say "I'll answer tomorrow"?

## Layoffs: Companies Should Tell You Early · 裁员应该提前告知

- Have you or a friend ever been laid off? How did you hear the news?
- Is it better to know three months early or one week early?
- Should a company tell staff before the news goes online?
- What should a company give you when you leave: money, time, or help finding a job?
- Would you rather take a pay cut, or watch ten people lose their jobs?

## The 35-Year-Old Hiring Cutoff Is Real · 35 岁招聘门槛

- Have you seen a job ad with an age limit? What did you think?
- Is 35 really too old to learn a new tool or start a new job?
- What can a 40-year-old offer that a 25-year-old cannot?
- Why do some companies prefer younger workers? Are they right?
- Should age limits in job ads be against the law?

## The Four-Day Workweek Is the Future · 四天工作制

- Would you work four long days instead of five normal days?
- Would you take 20% less pay for one more free day?
- What would you do with an extra weekday off?
- Can a small company afford a four-day week?
- Which is better for the country: more free time or more output?

## Most Meetings Are a Waste of Time · 大多数会议都是浪费时间

- How many hours of meetings do you have in a week?
- Which meeting was a total waste of your time? What happened?
- Could that meeting have been a message instead?
- Do you speak in meetings, or just listen with your camera off?
- What is one rule that would make meetings shorter?

## Remote Workers Are More Productive · 远程办公效率更高

- Do you get more done at home or in the office?
- How much time do you spend commuting every day?
- Is it easy to stay focused when your family is at home?
- Should remote workers be paid less than office workers?
- How can a manager know you are working, if they cannot see you?

## Casual Dress Codes Beat Formal Ones · 便装比正装好

- What do you wear to work now? What did people wear ten years ago?
- Do you feel more professional in a suit or in a T-shirt?
- Should a client-facing job have a stricter dress code?
- Have you ever been told your clothes were not okay for work?
- Does what you wear change how you work?

## Employees Should Challenge the Boss · 员工应该挑战老板

- Have you ever disagreed with your manager in a meeting?
- Is it safe to say "I don't agree" to your boss?
- What is a polite way to disagree with a superior?
- Is a quiet team a happy team, or a scared team?
- Should a boss ever say "you were right, I was wrong"?
