# Photography

**Types of Photos**

- What kind of photos do you take most — people, streets, food, or nature?
- Do you prefer taking photos of people or of objects and places? Why?
- Have you ever tried street photography, catching strangers in a moment?
- Whose photos do you like online — portraits, landscapes, or food?
- Have you ever tried wildlife or macro photography? What was it like?
- Is a portrait of a person harder to take than a photo of a view?
- What is a type of photography you have never tried but want to?

**Techniques and Gear**

- Do you shoot on a phone or a camera? Which do you reach for and why?
- How do you choose what to photograph when you are on a trip?
- Do you know the rules of composition, like the rule of thirds?
- How important is good light to a photo? When do you like to shoot?
- Have you ever bought gear you barely used? What was it?
- What makes a photo look professional to you?
- Do you take many shots of the same thing, or just one or two?

**Editing and Filters**

- Do you edit your photos before you post them? How much is too much?
- Do filters change the "truth" of a photo? Does that matter to you?
- Have you ever edited a photo so much it barely looked like you?
- Do you keep the original file, or delete it after you edit?
- What makes an edited photo look fake to you?
- Would you ever pay for a photo app or a filter pack? Why?
- Do you edit photos for your family, or only for yourself?

**Photos and Stories**

- What role does photography play in telling a story?
- Can you think of a photo that changed how people saw an event?
- How do photos shape the way we remember a trip or a person?
- Is it rude to photograph people without asking? Why or why not?
- Do people your age take too many photos instead of enjoying the moment?
- How do you feel when a stranger photographs you on the street?
- Can a single photo change how you feel about a place?

**Photos and Culture**

- How does photography show what daily life is like in your city?
- What do tourists always photograph here, and what do locals never do?
- How has phone photography changed the way we travel?
- Do people post photos more for themselves or for the likes?
- How is a formal photo, like a wedding photo, different from a daily one?
- What old family photos do you have, and what do they show?
- How do you think photos will change in the next ten years?

**Your Own Photos**

- What inspired you to start taking photos?
- What is your favorite photo that you have taken? Describe it.
- Do you print any of your photos, or keep them all on your phone?
- Do you have a photo that means a lot to your whole family?
- If you could photograph anywhere in the world, where would you go?
- What would you like to learn about photography in the next year?
- Who is the best photographer you know personally?
