**Conversation Questions for A2 Pre-Intermediate (1500 Questions) - ESL
Conversation Questions**

**Family**

- How many people are there in your immediate family? Can you tell me
  their names and ages?

- Do you have any siblings? If yes, how many and what are their names?
  If no, would you like to have siblings?

- Who do you resemble the most in your family, physically or in terms of
  personality?

- What activities do you enjoy doing with your family? Can you give me
  some examples?

- Do you have any family traditions or rituals that you follow? Can you
  describe one of them?

- What is your favorite memory with your family? Why is it special to
  you?

- Is there a family member who has had a significant impact on your
  life? How have they influenced you?

- What do you think makes a family strong and united? Can you give some
  examples?

- How often do you have family meals together? What do you usually talk
  about during those meals?

- Are you close to your extended family, like grandparents, aunts, or
  uncles? How often do you see them?

- What do you appreciate the most about your parents or guardians? Can
  you share a specific quality or trait?

- Are there any family traditions or customs from your culture that you
  think are unique or interesting?

- Do you have any family rules or expectations that you need to follow?
  What are they?

- How do you celebrate birthdays in your family? Do you have any special
  traditions for birthdays?

- If you could change one thing about your family, what would it be and
  why?

**Hobbies**

- What are your favorite hobbies? Why do you enjoy doing them?

- How often do you engage in your hobbies? Do you have a specific
  schedule or do you do them whenever you have free time?

- Have you ever tried any new hobbies? If so, what were they and why did
  you decide to try them?

- Do you prefer indoor or outdoor hobbies? Can you give me some examples
  of each?

- Are there any hobbies you would like to learn in the future? What
  interests you about those hobbies?

- Do you like to do hobbies alone or with others? Why?

- Have you ever taught someone else how to do one of your hobbies? How
  was the experience?

- Are there any hobbies that you used to do but no longer do? Why did
  you stop doing them?

- Do you collect anything as a hobby? If yes, what do you collect and
  why?

- How do your hobbies help you relax and de-stress after a long day?

- Have you ever participated in any competitions or events related to
  your hobbies? Can you share your experience?

- Are there any famous people who share the same hobbies as you? Who are
  they and what hobbies do they have?

- Do you think it's important for everyone to have hobbies? Why or why
  not?

- Have you ever tried combining two or more hobbies together? How did
  that turn out?

- If you had unlimited time and resources, what new hobby would you like
  to try?

  How do you prepare for a trip? Do you make a list or plan an itinerary

  Is there a destination you dream of visiting in the future? Why does

  Do you think it's important to learn some basic phrases in the local

  photos, write journals, or collect souvenirs?
