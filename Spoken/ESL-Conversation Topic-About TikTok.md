**Discussion**

- What is TikTok, and why do you think it is so popular?
- Have you ever posted a video on Douyin or TikTok? How did it go?
- What do you think makes a short video go viral?
- How has short video changed the way we relax and spend free time?
- Can you learn anything useful from TikTok? Give an example.
- How do you feel about the privacy rules on these apps?
- Why do short videos hold your attention better than long ones?
- Which age group uses TikTok the most, and why?
- What should a person be careful about when using TikTok?
- If you wanted to make a viral video, what would you do?
- How much time do you spend scrolling short videos in a day?
- Do you ever buy things after seeing them in a video? What was it?
- Have you ever copied a dance, a recipe, or a life hack from TikTok?
- How is Douyin different from TikTok? Which one do you use?
- Should there be a time limit for short-video apps? Why or why not?
- What would your life be like without short videos for a week?
- Have you ever been annoyed by a video that everyone was sharing?
- Who is the funniest creator you follow, and what do they post?
- Do you trust the advice you see in short videos? Why or why not?
- How do you think short video will change entertainment in ten years?
