# Managing Up

**Talking to Your Leader**
- What does "managing up" mean to you?
- How often do you report to your leader: in person, email, or an app?
- Are you more task-focused or more people-focused? What about your leader?
- Is your leader hands-on and attentive, or do they prefer a self-driven team?
- Does your communication style match your leader's: direct or indirect?
- What about working style: teamwork or solo, one task or many at once?
- Is it ever a good idea to be friends with your leader? Where is the line?

**What Slows You Down**
- Lack of clarity · time pressure · too few resources
- Overlapping responsibilities · procrastination · shifting priorities
- Skill gaps · personality clashes
- Which of these slows you down most, and how do you get past it?
- Do you ask your leader for help? What exactly do you say?

**What You Need From Your Leader**
- Clear instructions · recognition and appreciation
- Trust and autonomy · constructive feedback · space to grow
- Which of these do you need most from your leader right now?

**When Things Go Wrong**
- Micromanagement · unrealistic expectations · underdelivering
- Inconsistent decisions · overpromising · results-only feedback
- How do you deal with each of these situations?

**If You Lead a Team**
- What do you not want to hear from your team?
- Too much detail · problems with no solutions · a passive attitude
- Excuses · office cliques · broken promises
- Have you ever had a conflict with your leader? How did you handle it?
- What is the single biggest thing you want from your leader?
