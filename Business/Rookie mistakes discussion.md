# Rookie Mistakes

**First Jobs**

- What was your first job, and how did you get it?
- How did you prepare for that job? What worried you the most back then?
- Was it the money, the commute, the rent, or something else that mattered most?
- Did your college major relate to your first job? What about your current job?
- What did you think work would be like before your first day? Were you right?
- Who helped you most in your first month, and what did they do?
- What do you know now that you wish you had known then?

**Starting Out**

- Do you feel safe in your workplace? Is it OK to make mistakes there?
- How did you feel the first time you made a mistake at work?
- Who do you turn to when you are stuck — a colleague, your manager, or a friend?
- In your field, is it better to ask a question or to figure it out alone?
- How does your company treat new hires in their first week?
- What is one small thing that makes a new job easier?

**Rookie Mistakes**

- What are some common rookie mistakes in your field?
- Do new hires ask too many questions, or do they act like they know it all?
- Do rookies sometimes over-explain everything they do? Why?
- Why do new hires answer "yes" or "no" before they think?
- Is oversharing personal information a problem at work? Have you seen it?
- Is being too eager to impress a mistake? What can go wrong?
- Which rookie mistake do you find the most forgivable, and why?

**Mistakes and Consequences**

- Which mistakes are acceptable in a job, and which ones are not?
- Can a small mistake ever cost a company a lot of money? Give an example.
- When the mistake is your fault, should you speak up right away or fix it first?
- How should a manager react when a new hire makes a mistake?
- Is it worse to hide a mistake or to make one in the first place?
- Do people learn more from their own mistakes or from other people's?

**Learning from Mistakes**

- What was the very first mistake you made in your career? How did you fix it?
- Did anyone help you fix it, or did you handle it alone?
- Did you report it right away, or try to solve it yourself first?
- Have you seen a newbie make the same mistake you once made? What did you do?
- What is the biggest lesson a mistake has taught you?
- How do you stop yourself from repeating the same mistake?
- Is there a mistake you are still embarrassed about today?

**Helping New Colleagues**

- If you were a senior mentor, how would you help rookies settle in?
- What could your company do to make a new hire's first month easier?
- Should a mentor step in early, or let a rookie learn the hard way?
- How do you give feedback to a new colleague without hurting them?
- What advice would you give someone starting in your field next week?
- If a new hire asks you the same question twice, how do you react?
