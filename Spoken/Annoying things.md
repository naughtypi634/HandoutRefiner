# Annoying Things

**Talking About Annoyance 表达烦躁**

- **It drives me crazy when ...** — 当……的时候我都快疯了
- **I can't stand it when ...** — 我最受不了……
- **It's so annoying when ...** — ……真是太让人烦了
- **What really bugs me is ...** — 真正让我烦恼的是……

**On the Move 出行**

- Subway rush hour 地铁早高峰: packed so tight you can't reach your phone
- Someone playing videos on speaker 外放刷视频: full volume, right next to you
- Shared bike is gone 共享单车不见了: the app says one is here, but it isn't
- Spring Festival tickets 春运抢票: sold out the second they go on sale
- The elevator stops on every floor 电梯层层停

**At Home 居家**

- Renovation upstairs 楼上装修: drilling starts at 7 a.m. on a Sunday
- Delivery at the wrong door 外卖送错: the photo shows an unknown door
- The parcel locker charges you 快递柜超时费: one yuan because you were at work
- The landlord wants more rent 房东涨租
- The drain is clogged again 下水道又堵了

**Phone and Internet 手机与网络**

- Spam calls 骚扰电话: insurance, loans, and fake delivery calls
- The work group chat 工作群: a message at 11 p.m., every single night
- @everyone in the group 群里@所有人: for something not about you
- Left on read 已读不回: read at noon, answered at midnight
- Ads before the video 广告太长: 60 seconds before you can skip

**Food and Shopping 吃喝购物**

- Takeout missing items 外卖漏送: no chopsticks, or no main dish
- Packaging you can't open 撕不开的包装: you need scissors for a yogurt
- The line for milk tea 奶茶排队: 40 minutes for one cup
- The line at the checkout 结账排队
- Double 11 parcels 双十一快递: twelve boxes and one is missing

**Social and Everyday 社交与日常**

- Small talk in the elevator 电梯尬聊: the same three questions every day
- Plans changed at the last minute 临时改计划
- Noise downstairs 楼下噪音: mahjong until 2 a.m.
- Rain on your day off 休息日下雨
- Mosquitoes at night 半夜蚊子

**Discussion 讨论**

- What is the most annoying thing about your commute?
- Which is worse: a loud phone or a slow elevator?
- What do you do when someone cuts in line?
- Have you ever lost your temper over something small?
- What noise in your neighborhood drives you crazy?
- Do you complain about bad service or let it go?
- What small thing ruined your day this week?
