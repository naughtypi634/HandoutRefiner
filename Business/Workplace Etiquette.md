# Workplace Etiquette

**Respect and Courtesy**

- Treat everyone with respect, whatever their job title.
- Listen actively: keep eye contact and don't interrupt.
- Show empathy — try to see things from their side.
- Respect personal space and privacy.
- Avoid gossip and negative talk.

**Communication**

- Be clear, concise, and polite, in speech and in writing.
- Use appropriate language — no slang or rude words.
- Reply to emails and messages promptly.
- Proofread before you send.
- Write clear subject lines and proper greetings.

**Professionalism and Appearance**

- Follow the dress code and look neat.
- Keep good personal hygiene.
- Keep your workspace clean and tidy.
- Be on time for work and meetings.

**Meetings and Shared Spaces**

- Arrive on time and ready to take part.
- Put your phone away and stay on the agenda.
- Clean up after yourself in the kitchen.
- Keep your voice down near other people's desks.

**Teamwork**

- Share credit for good work.
- Offer help to colleagues when they need it.
- Work together toward shared goals.

**Communication — Don'ts**

- Don't take personal calls on speaker in the open office.
- Don't interrupt people while they're talking.
- Don't spread rumors or talk badly about colleagues.
- Don't ignore emails and messages — reply.
- Don't hit "reply all" unless everyone needs to know.
- Don't overshare personal details.
- Don't use bad language.

**Time and Boundaries — Don'ts**

- Don't show up late again and again.
- Don't scroll your phone in meetings.
- Don't micromanage — trust your team.
- Don't take credit for someone else's work.

**Shared Spaces — Don'ts**

- Don't leave a mess in shared areas.
- Don't microwave strong-smelling food at lunch.
- Don't forget to mute in online meetings.

**Personal Habits — Don'ts**

- Don't skip basic hygiene.
- Don't chew loudly.
