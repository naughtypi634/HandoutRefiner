# Phone Addiction

**Your Phone Time**

- How much time do you spend on your phone every day?
- What is your screen time this week? Was it higher than you thought?
- When do you use your phone — mornings, meals, or before bed?
- How often do you check your phone? What makes you check it?
- Do you check your phone first thing after you wake up?
- Does your phone ever stop you from finishing work or study?
- Do you feel uneasy when your phone battery is low?

**Which Apps Eat Your Day**

- Which apps do you use the most? Name three.
- How much short video do you watch in a day?
- Which app keeps you up at night?
- Do you shop, order food, or pay on your phone? How often?
- How many group chats are you in? Which one never stops?
- Which app makes you feel tired or stressed?
- Which app could you delete today and not miss?

**Friends Online and Offline**

- Can you make "real" friends on social media? Have you?
- Do you prefer online chats or meeting in person? Why?
- How many of your contacts have you actually met face to face?
- Do you compare your life to other people's posts? How does it feel?
- Do you reply to messages right away, or leave people on read?
- Is a "like" as good as a real compliment? Why or why not?
- Have you ever met an online friend in person? How did it go?

**Life Before Smartphones**

- Do you remember life before smartphones? What was different?
- How did you arrange to meet friends before smartphones?
- What did you do while eating, waiting, or riding the subway back then?
- Is life better or worse now? Give a real example.
- What do you miss most about the days without them?
- How did your parents spend their free time before phones?
- Could you go a whole day without your phone? What would happen?

**Too Much of a Good Thing**

- What is the best thing your smartphone has brought you?
- What is the worst thing it has brought you?
- Do you agree that a phone is "a double-edged sword"?
- When did you last feel you had to cut down your screen time?
- Have you ever tried a screen-time limit or a phone-free hour?
- How do you fight phone addiction in yourself?
- What rule would you set for children and their phones?

**Phone Habits at Home**

- Do you use your phone at the dinner table? Does anyone complain?
- Do you watch a show with one eye on your phone?
- Has a phone ever ruined a perfect moment for you? What happened?
- Do you keep your phone by your bed at night? Why?
- What do you do to relax without your phone?
