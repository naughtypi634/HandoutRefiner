**About TikTok/Red/Other social media platforms**

TikTok is a **global** social media **platform** where **users** share
short, creative videos. It **covers** ***[a range of]{.underline}***
topics, from music, dance and comedy, to educational **content**.
TikTok's **unique**, **quick-paced** **format** keeps users
**entertained** and **engaged**.
