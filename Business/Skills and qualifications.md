# Skills and Qualifications

**Education and Training**
- What was your major, and why did you choose it?
- Which class or teacher actually stuck with you, and what did they teach you?
- Was your school better at teaching theory or at preparing you for a real job?
- How much of what you studied do you still use at work today?
- Did you do an internship, and how did it compare with your classes?
- Looking back, what would you have studied differently?
- Is a degree from a top university still worth the pressure in 2026?

**Skills Employers Ask For**
- Think of the last job ad you read. Which skills kept appearing?
- Which of those skills does your own job actually need every day?
- Are these skills usually written as soft or hard skills in the ad? Why?
- If you could only list three skills on your resume, which three and why?
- Which skill do Chinese employers value more than foreign ones do?
- Which skill is easiest to prove in an interview, and how would you prove it?
- Do certificates and scores really show how good you are at a skill?

**Your Strong Points and Weak Spots**
- What are you genuinely good at, and how did you find out?
- Name one skill you are proud of. How did you build it?
- What is one weakness you are working on right now?
- How would a close colleague describe your strongest skill?
- Is there a skill everyone expects you to have but you actually lack?
- How do you respond when a job asks for a skill you don't have yet?

**Learning New Skills**
- How do you prefer to learn: hands-on practice, a course, a tutor, or videos?
- What skill did you teach yourself, and what made you stick with it?
- How much time can you honestly spend learning after a long workday?
- Do you learn faster from a mentor or from your own mistakes? Give an example.
- Which skill do you want to learn this year, and what is stopping you?
- How has AI changed the way you pick up a new skill?

**Study vs Work Experience**
- Which teaches you more about a real job: university or the first year at work?
- What do you make of everyone rushing to get an MBA these days?
- Two years of work experience or a master's overseas — which would you choose?
- Does your company actually pay more for a higher degree?
- Is further study worth it if you have to stop working and lose income?
- Which jobs in your field truly need a master's, and which just list it?
- If your company offered paid study leave, what would you study?

**Training at Work**
- Does your company train new hires properly, or is it sink-or-swim?
- Which job do you think needs the most training before day one?
- Do you think all jobs need training? Why or why not?
- When training is boring, how do you keep yourself engaged?
- How would you redesign onboarding for your own company?
- What is one thing your manager could teach you if you asked?
