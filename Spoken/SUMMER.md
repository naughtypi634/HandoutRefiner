# Summer

**Summer Memories**

- What comes to mind when you hear the word "summer"?
- What do you remember about the summer holidays when you were young?
- What did you do in summer as a student that you can't do now?
- What summer activity did you love most as a child?
- Did summer feel longer when you were young? Why?
- What is your earliest memory of a summer day?
- Where did your family go for summer when you were small?

**Seasons and Feelings**

- Which season do you like the most? Share your ranking and why.
- Do you prefer summer or winter? Why?
- How does the weather change your mood? Give an example.
- Does summer mean something different now that you are not a student?
- What does summer mean for people who work full-time?
- Do you like long, bright evenings? Why or why not?
- How do you feel when summer first arrives each year?

**Food and Drink**

- What is your go-to summer food or drink?
- What do you eat more of in summer than in any other season?
- Do you like cold noodles, ice cream, or watermelon most?
- What is the best drink on a hot afternoon? Why?
- Is there a summer dish your family always makes?
- Do you drink more coffee or bubble tea in summer?
- What summer snack did you love as a child?

**Getting Out and Travel**

- What is the best travel destination in summer? Why?
- Do you travel more in summer, or stay home? Why?
- How do you stay cool on a hot day out?
- What outdoor activity is best in summer?
- Do you go to the beach, the mountains, or the pool?
- What is the busiest place in your city in summer?
- How do you plan a trip during the summer rush?

**The Downside of Summer**

- What are some downsides of summer?
- How do you deal with the heat on a really bad day?
- Does summer make you lazy or tired? Why?
- What do you dislike about summer crowds?
- Have you ever been sunburned? What happened?
- Do mosquitoes ruin your summer nights? What do you do?
- What is the worst part of a long, hot summer for you?

**Summer Today**

- How do you spend a normal summer weekend now?
- What is one thing you want to do every summer but never do?
- How has summer changed in your city over the years?
- What would your perfect summer day look like?
- What do you look forward to most about summer this year?
