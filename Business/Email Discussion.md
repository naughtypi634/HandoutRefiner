# Email Discussion

**Everyday Email**

- How often do you check your email — hourly, daily, or rarely?
- Do you read email on your phone or on a computer? Which is easier?
- Do you keep one email address for work and another for yourself?
- Have you ever paid for extra storage or security for your email?
- What is the first email you open in the morning?
- Do you answer emails right away, or leave them for later?
- How many unread emails are sitting in your inbox right now?

**Managing the Inbox**

- How do you organize your inbox — folders, labels, or nothing at all?
- What do you do when your inbox has 200 unread emails?
- How long does it take you to write an important email?
- Do you use templates for emails you send again and again?
- How do you deal with spam and emails you never asked for?
- Do you delete emails, archive them, or just let them pile up?
- Have you ever missed something important in a crowded inbox?

**Messages That Matter**

- Do you prefer email or WeChat for work? Why?
- Which emails do you open and answer first? How do you decide?
- How do you write an email that is professional but still friendly?
- Do you prefer short emails or long, detailed ones?
- Should you answer work email at night or on the weekend?
- Do you get annoyed when someone replies only "OK" to a long email?
- Why is email still so important in the workplace?

**Writing Well**

- What makes an email easy to answer? What makes it annoying?
- Do you re-read an important email before you send it?
- Have you ever sent an email to the wrong person? What happened?
- Is it okay to use emojis in a work email? When?
- How do you politely ask someone to reply faster?
- What is a good subject line for a meeting request?

**Problems**

- Have you ever been in a chain of 30 emails about one small thing?
- Do you use "reply all" a lot, or only when you must?
- What do you do when an email is confusing or badly written?
- Have you ever written an angry email, then deleted it?
- What is the worst work-email mistake you have heard of?
- How do you follow up when someone never replies?
- Would you rather call a person or send an email about a problem?

**Email and Me**

- Do you feel stressed when your inbox fills up? Why?
- Could you go one whole day without checking email?
- Is email making people lazier at talking face to face?
- What email habit of yours would you like to change?
- Do you keep old emails, or clean them out often?
- If email disappeared tomorrow, what would you use instead?
