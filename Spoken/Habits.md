# Habits

**Your Daily Routine**

- What do you always do in the first ten minutes after you wake up?
- What do you usually do during your lunch break?
- Do you drink tea, coffee, or alcohol? How often do you have one?
- Are you usually early, on time, or late for meetings?
- Do you have a habit of checking your phone? How many times a day?
- What do you do on the metro every morning — music, videos, or sleep?
- Do you ever swear? Are you a good listener when other people talk?

**Habits and Health**

- Would you say your eating habits are healthy? What did you eat yesterday?
- How are your sleeping habits? What time do you usually go to bed?
- What are your exercise habits? Do you get outdoors often?
- Do you order takeout most nights, or do you cook at home?
- What do you usually do when you feel worried or nervous?
- What good habits keep your brain active and healthy?
- What habits would you suggest to someone who wants more happiness?

**Money and Shopping Habits**

- Do you check prices online before you buy something in a shop?
- How often do you buy things you never use? What was the last one?
- Do you save money every month, or spend whatever is left?
- Are you a "add to cart and forget it" person, or a careful buyer?
- What is one subscription you pay for but barely use?
- Do you compare prices on two or three apps before ordering food?

**Work Habits**

- Do you make a to-do list? Do you actually finish it?
- Do you check WeChat or email the moment you wake up?
- What do you do first when you sit down at your desk?
- Do you finish one task before starting another, or do several at once?
- How do you keep track of deadlines — an app, a notebook, or your memory?
- What is a work habit you would like your colleagues to change?

**Habits Around You**

- Does a friend or family member have a strange habit? What is it?
- What habit of other people irritates you most? Why does it annoy you?
- Does anyone you know always check their phone during dinner?
- Did you have different habits when you were younger? What were they?
- What habit have you had all your life? Is it a good one?
- Do you know anyone who managed to change a habit? How did they do it?
- Can you think of a good habit that helps people succeed?

**Making and Breaking Habits**

- What is a bad habit you would like to stop? How can you stop it?
- What is a habit you want to develop? How will you make it stick?
- What is the difference between a habit and a hobby?
- How long must you do something daily before it becomes a habit?
- Where do you think your habits came from?
- Why is it so hard to keep a new habit after the first week?
- Have you ever tried a 21-day or 30-day habit challenge? Did it work?
- What would be a very difficult habit to stop? Why is it so hard?
