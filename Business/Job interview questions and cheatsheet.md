# Job Interview Questions

**Warm-Up**
- Which interview questions have you heard of before? List as many as you can.
- Which of them would be hardest for you? Rank your top three.
- What do you think the interviewer is really asking with each one?

**The Ten Most Common Questions**
- Can you tell me about yourself?
- Why are you leaving your current position?
- Why do you want to work here?
- What are your motivations for this role?
- Why should we hire you?
- What is your greatest achievement?
- What is your dream job?
- If you could be any animal, what would you be?
- How do you manage tight deadlines at work?
- Where do you see yourself in five years?

**Questions About Your Past**
- What did you like most about your last job?
- Why did you leave your last two positions?
- What was the hardest project you have worked on?
- What would your last boss say about you?
- Which job taught you the most, and why?
- Have you ever been let go from a job, and what did you learn?
- What do you miss about your old team?

**Tricky Situations**
- What is a mistake you made at work, and how did you fix it?
- How do you handle a boss who gives unclear instructions?
- What do you do when a client changes the deadline at the last minute?
- Have you ever disagreed with a team decision? What did you do?
- How do you decide when three people all say their task is urgent?
- What would you do if a coworker took credit for your idea?
- How do you stay calm when an interviewer keeps pushing back?

**Questions About the Company**
- What do you know about our company and our main product?
- Why this industry, and why now?
- Who do you think our biggest competitor is?
- How would you improve something we do?
- What part of this job are you most excited about?
- Where else are you interviewing right now?

**Your Turn to Ask**
- What does a typical day in this role look like?
- How will you measure my performance in the first year?
- What is the team culture like on a busy week?
- What are the next steps, and what is the timeline?
- Who would I report to, and how often?
- What happened to the person who had this job before?
- Is there anything about my background you would like me to explain?
