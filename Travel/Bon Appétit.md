# Bon Appétit

**Warm Up**

- What's your favorite type of cuisine, and why do you love it?
- Do you prefer dining in or ordering takeout? What's your go-to order?
- What's the best restaurant you've ever been to, and what made it special?
- If you could only eat one type of food for life, what would it be?
- What dish can you cook well? Who taught you how to make it?
- What do you usually eat for breakfast on a busy weekday morning?
- What food do people in your hometown eat that visitors always love?

**Restaurant Experiences**

- What's the most memorable dining experience you've had?
- Have you ever had a bad experience at a restaurant? What happened?
- What's your favorite restaurant in your city, and what do you order there?
- Do you enjoy trying new places, or do you stick to your favorites?
- How important is the atmosphere of a restaurant to your meal?
- Do you scan a QR code to order now? Do you like it or not?
- Have you ever sent a dish back to the kitchen? How did it go?

**Health**

- How do you balance enjoying food with keeping a healthy diet?
- What's your opinion on organic or locally grown food?
- Do you have any dietary restrictions or preferences? Why?
- How do you feel about meal prepping? Is it something you do?
- Have you ever eaten too much and felt bad later that night?
- Do you read the labels when you buy snacks or drinks? Why?
- What is one small change that made your diet healthier?

**Food Favorites and Opinions**

- What food do you find underrated or overrated?
- Are there any foods you absolutely refuse to eat? Why?
- How do you feel about plant-based or vegan diets?
- What's your favorite meal of the day: breakfast, lunch, or dinner?
- How do you feel about buffets? Do you enjoy them or find them too much?
- Do you eat to live, or live to eat? Which one are you?
- What is a dish from another country you could eat every week?

**Food Delivery and Technology**

- What's your opinion on food delivery apps? Do you use them often?
- How do you feel about ordering through an app inside a restaurant?
- How long do you wait for delivery before you cancel the order?
- Have you ever left a one-star review for cold or late food? Why?
- Do delivery riders get a fair deal from the apps? What could change?
- Would you pay more for delivery so riders earn a better wage?
- Do you trust the photos and ratings on a delivery app? Why or why not?

**Food Memories**

- What was your favorite food memory from childhood?
- Have you ever traveled somewhere just for the food? Where did you go?
- What dish reminds you of your grandmother's kitchen?
- Who in your family is the best cook, and what is their best dish?
- What food did you hate as a child but love now?
- Is there a dish you only eat at a festival or a family event?
