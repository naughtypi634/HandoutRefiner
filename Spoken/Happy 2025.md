# Going Home for Spring Festival

**Getting Home**

- How often do you go back to your hometown? Is it a long trip?
- Did you grab your ticket the second it went on sale?
- Do you take the high-speed rail, a plane, or drive home?
- How do you pass the time on the train — sleep, snacks, or short videos?
- What is in your suitcase for the family this year?
- Do you travel home alone, or with your partner or friends?
- What is the first thing you do when you walk in the door?

**Back Home Again**

- How many days did you stay this year? Was it long enough?
- Did your daily routine change at home — late nights, big breakfasts?
- Was anything different about the house or the street this year?
- Did you sleep well at home, or worse than in your own bed?
- What did you do to relax while you were back?
- Did you help with cooking or cleaning, or just rest?
- Did you feel like a guest, or like you still lived there?

**Family and Old Friends**

- Who did you spend the most time with while you were home?
- Did relatives ask the usual questions — salary, marriage, or a baby?
- Did you meet any old classmates or childhood friends?
- Did you reconnect with someone you had not seen for years?
- Was there a family member you were really happy to see?
- Did a small argument happen at the table, the way it always does?
- Who in the family changed the most since you last saw them?

**New Year Traditions**

- What is on your family's New Year dinner table? Who cooks it?
- Did you set off fireworks, or just watch the neighbors do it?
- Did you watch the Spring Festival Gala this year? Which part did you like?
- Did you join the Spring Festival film rush? Which movie did you pick?
- Did you give or get red packets? Who got the most this year?
- What tradition does your family keep, no matter what?

**Hometown Now**

- Is your hometown changing? What is new since last year?
- Are there new shops, a new subway line, or new buildings?
- Did you visit your old school or your old neighborhood?
- What do you miss most about your hometown when you are away?
- Is daily life there slower or faster than in the city?
- Would you ever bring a friend from the city to visit?

**Looking Ahead**

- Would you live there again one day, or stay in the city?
- Do you think your hometown is still a part of who you are?
- Do your parents want you to come back more often? Do you?
- What do you want to plan better next Spring Festival?
- What did this trip make you think about your own future?
- What is one wish you have for the new year?
