# Business Discussion Questions

**Work & Money**

- What matters most in a job — money, enjoying your work, or what else?
- How do we find a good job? Apps, referrals, job fairs, or any other way?
- What helps people get a good job — a degree, real skills, work experience, or anything else?

**Work Style & Work-Life Balance**

- What company do you want to work for — a big one, a small one, or another kind?
- What makes a meeting useful — a clear goal, a short time, the right people, or any other ideas?
- How can people balance work and family — flexible hours, family help, or what else?
- What is the best way to show you work hard — long hours, good ideas, or what else?

**Technology & the Future of Work**

- How should companies sell — on WeChat, on live-streaming, in shops, or somewhere else?
- What will work look like — home offices, big offices, or something completely different?
- How do you deal with work messages after work — reply right away, wait, or ignore them?
- How will AI change work — help us, take our jobs, or both?

**Company Size & Organization**

- What helps a company react fast — small teams, good leaders, or what else?
- How do people get a promotion — hard work, good relationships, or any other way?

**Sales & Customers**

- Who is a salesperson — the sales team, customer service, delivery riders, or everyone?
- What do good salespeople need — product knowledge, a friendly manner, or any other skills?

**Business Ethics & Fairness**

- What should customers do about cheating companies — complain, stop buying, or post online?
- When is it OK for a company to pay less tax — never, in hard times, or any other time?
- How is work different for men and women — pay, promotions, job types, or anything else?
