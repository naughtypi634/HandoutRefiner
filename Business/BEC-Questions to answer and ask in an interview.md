# Interview Questions to Ask and Answer

**Company Questions**
- What does the management and organizational structure look like here?
- Where do you see the company in the next five to seven years?
- What do you like most and least about working here?
- What is the company culture like in practice, not just on the website?
- What is the biggest challenge facing the company right now?
- Who is your biggest competitor, and what do you each do better?
- If you could change one thing about this company, what would it be?

**Position Questions**
- What would my work schedule look like?
- What kind of work should I expect in the first year?
- Can you describe a typical day, week, and month in this position?
- Is this a new position, or am I replacing someone?
- Could I talk with the last person in this role, or with one of their peers?
- What are the main challenges someone in this position should expect?
- How does this role contribute to the team and the company overall?

**Training, Pay, and Next Steps**
- How will my performance in this position be measured?
- What training do you offer for new hires and for current staff?
- What career paths are open to people in this position?
- What benefits do you offer: bonuses, vacation, sick leave, insurance?
- Are people allowed to work remotely, and how much travel is expected?
- Are there gaps in my qualifications that I could still improve on?
- What are the next steps, and when will I hear back?

**About You**
- Tell me about yourself — what should I know first?
- What are your strengths, and what is your biggest weakness?
- Are you willing to travel or to relocate?
- What sets you apart from the other candidates?
- Why should we hire you?
- Are you more detail-oriented or more of a big-picture person?
- Do you prefer working alone or as part of a team?

**Your Work History**
- What are your career goals?
- What is your greatest professional achievement, and how did you get there?
- What would you like to be doing in five years?
- What did you like and dislike about your last boss?
- Which career rewards matter most to you?
- Why are you leaving your current job?
- What was your biggest accomplishment in your last job?

**Behavioral Questions**
- Can you give an example of a time you went above and beyond to get a job done?
- Describe a time you could not meet a deadline. How did you handle it?
- Have you ever been under pressure to meet many deadlines at once?
- How have you handled a conflict with a coworker?
- Can you give an example of a mistake you made, and how you fixed it?
- Has an assignment ever been too hard for you? What did you do?
- What idea did you have at work, and how did you put it into action?
