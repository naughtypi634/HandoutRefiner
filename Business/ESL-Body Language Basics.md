# Body Language Basics

**Why It Matters**

| **Reason** | **Detail** |
| --- | --- |
| First impressions | We judge a stranger's character from body language. |
| No words needed | We use it to communicate when we can't speak. |
| Hiring | Employers and interviewers watch it closely. |

**Facial Expressions**

| **Expression** | **Meaning** |
| --- | --- |
| Avoiding eye contact | Shy, worried, or lying |
| A blank face | Hiding feelings, or no emotion |
| Direct eye contact | Confidence |
| Eyes looking into the distance | Daydreaming, not focused |
| Pressed lips (tight-lipped) | Annoyed or angry |
| Raised eyebrows | Doubtful or surprised |
| Smiling | Friendly |
| A fake smile (mouth only) | Being polite, not really happy |

**Posture and Stance**

| **Posture** | **Meaning** |
| --- | --- |
| Standing tall, shoulders back | Confident and in control |
| Slouching in the chair | Tired, bored, or unsure |
| Sitting on the edge of the seat | Keen and interested, or nervous |
| Leaning forward | Listening closely |
| Turning the body away | Wanting to end the conversation |

**Hand Gestures**

| **Gesture** | **Meaning** |
| --- | --- |
| Hands behind the back, shoulders back | Confident |
| Arms crossed | Defensive or insecure (sometimes angry) |
| Open palms, facing up | Honest and open |
| Both palms forward | Showing submission, or "stop" |
| Pointing with the index finger | Aggressive or accusing |
| Scratching the head | Confused |
| Shrugging shoulders | "I don't know", or unsure |
| Stroking the chin | Thinking deeply |

**Nervous Habits**

| **Habit** | **Meaning** |
| --- | --- |
| Biting the nails | Nervous |
| Blushing | Embarrassed |
| Covering an open mouth | Scared or shocked |
| Rubbing the eyes | Tired, or not believing something |
| Playing with a pen or phone | Bored, or anxious |

**Personal Space**

| **Distance** | **Meaning** |
| --- | --- |
| Standing under 45 cm away | Intimate — only for close friends or family |
| Standing 45–120 cm away | Personal space — normal for a friendly chat |
| Keeping 1.2–3 m apart | Social distance — for business and strangers |
| Stepping back as you move closer | Feeling uncomfortable or pressured |
| Standing shoulder to shoulder | Working together on the same task |

**Listening and Responding**

| **Signal** | **Meaning** |
| --- | --- |
| Nodding slowly | Agreement, or "yes" |
| Shaking the head | Saying "no" |
| Tilting the head to one side | Curious, or listening hard |
| Cupping a hand behind the ear | "I can't hear you" |
| Glancing at the clock or phone | Bored, or in a hurry |

**Meeting and Greeting**

| **Action** | **Meaning** |
| --- | --- |
| Firm handshake | Strong and confident |
| Limp handshake | Weak or uninterested |
| A two-handed handshake | Warm, but can feel too strong |
| A slight bow | A greeting in many countries |
| Offering a business card with both hands | Polite and respectful |
| Waiting for the senior person to sit first | Respect for age and rank |
| Checking your watch during a meeting | Rude — you look bored |
| Leaving your phone face-up on the table | Distracted, not fully present |
