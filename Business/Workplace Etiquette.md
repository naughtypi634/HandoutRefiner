**The Dos**

**1. Respect and Courtesy:**

- **Treat everyone with respect:** This means being polite, considerate,
  and professional in all interactions, regardless of job title or
  position.

- **Listen actively:** Pay attention to what others are saying, maintain
  eye contact, and avoid interrupting.

- **Show empathy:** Try to understand and appreciate different
  perspectives.

- **Be mindful of personal space and boundaries:** Respect the physical
  space and privacy of colleagues.

- **Avoid gossip and negative talk:** Stick to professional
  conversations and refrain from spreading rumors. 

**2. Communication:**

- **Communicate effectively:** Be clear, concise, and respectful in both
  written and verbal communication.

- **Use appropriate language:** Avoid using slang, profanity, or
  disrespectful terms.

- **Respond promptly to emails and messages:** Acknowledge receipt and
  provide a timely response.

- **Proofread written communications:** Ensure that emails and other
  written materials are free of errors.

- **Master email etiquette:** Use clear subject lines, proper greetings
  and closings, and concise language. 

**3. Professionalism and Appearance:**

- **Dress appropriately:** Adhere to the workplace dress code and
  maintain a neat and professional appearance.

- **Maintain good personal hygiene:** Show up to work clean and
  well-groomed.

- **Keep your workspace clean and organized:** A tidy work area reflects
  professionalism.

- **Be punctual:** Arrive on time for work and meetings. 

**4. Meetings and Shared Spaces:**

- **Be punctual for meetings:** Arrive on time and be prepared to
  participate.

- **Minimize distractions:** Put away devices and focus on the meeting
  agenda.

- **Respect shared spaces:** Clean up after yourself in areas like the
  kitchen and meeting rooms.

- **Be mindful of noise levels:** Avoid loud phone conversations or
  distracting noises. 

**5. Teamwork and Collaboration:**

- **Share credit when appropriate:** Acknowledge the contributions of
  others.

- **Offer help to colleagues:** Support your team members and assist
  when needed.

- **Collaborate effectively:** Work together towards shared goals in a
  respectful manner.

**The Don'ts**

**1. Poor Communication and Interactions:**

- **Loud Talkers:** Taking personal phone calls loudly, using
  speakerphone in open offices, or just generally having a loud voice
  that disrupts others\' ability to concentrate.

- **Constant Interruptions:** Frequently interrupting colleagues during
  conversations or meetings, showing a lack of respect for their time
  and focus.

- **Gossiping and Negativity:** Spreading rumors, speaking negatively
  about colleagues or the company, and bringing down team morale.

- **Ignoring Messages or Being Unresponsive:** Being slow or failing to
  respond to emails and messages, which can hinder collaboration and
  productivity.

- **Unnecessary \"Reply All\" Emails:** Using \"reply all\"
  unnecessarily, clogging up inboxes and distracting colleagues.

- **Using Inappropriate Language:** Using profanity or informal language
  that is not suitable for a professional environment.

- **Oversharing Personal Information:** Sharing excessively personal
  details that can make colleagues uncomfortable. 

**2. Disrespecting Time and Boundaries:**

- **Chronic Tardiness:** Being consistently late to work or meetings,
  which can disrupt the flow of work and disrespect others\' time.

- **Not Paying Attention in Meetings:** Being distracted by phones,
  emails, or other tasks during meetings, showing a lack of engagement.

- **Micromanaging:** Excessive oversight and control over employees,
  which can be frustrating and hinder autonomy.

- **Stealing Credit:** Taking credit for others\' work, which undermines
  teamwork and trust. 

**3. Misusing Shared Spaces:**

- **Leaving a Mess:** Not cleaning up after oneself in shared areas like
  the kitchen or break room, showing a lack of consideration for others.

- **Eating Smelly Food:** Reheating or eating food with strong odors in
  shared spaces, which can be disruptive and unpleasant for colleagues.

- **Not Muting Background Noise in Virtual Meetings:** Disrupting online
  meetings with avoidable background noises. 

**4. Poor Personal Habits:**

- **Poor Hygiene:** Lack of cleanliness or grooming, which can be a
  significant annoyance for colleagues.

- **Loud Eating/Chewing:** Chewing loudly or making other distracting
  noises while eating.

- **Taking Off Shoes and Having Smelly Feet:** Disrespecting colleagues
  by not being mindful of foot odor. 

Workplace Etiquette

**The Dos (Good Things to Do)**

1.  **Be Respectful and Kind**

    - Treat everyone nicely: Be polite and professional with all
      coworkers, no matter their job.

    - Listen well: Look at people when they talk, and don\'t interrupt.

    - Understand others: Try to see things from their point of view.

    - Respect space: Stay out of people\'s personal space and privacy.

    - Avoid gossip: Don\'t spread rumors or talk negatively.

2.  **Communicate Nicely**

    - Be clear and kind: Speak and write in a simple, respectful way.

    - Use proper words: No slang, bad words, or rude terms.

    - ~~Reply quickly: Answer emails and messages soon.~~

3.  **Look and Act Professional**

    - Dress right: Follow the dress code and look neat.

    - Stay clean: Shower and groom yourself well.

    - Keep your desk tidy: A clean workspace looks professional.

    - Be on time: Arrive at work and meetings when you should.

4.  **In Meetings and Shared Areas**

    - Be on time for meetings: Show up ready to join in.

    - Focus: Put away phones and pay attention.

    - Clean up: Tidy shared spots like the kitchen.

    - Keep quiet: Don\'t make loud noises or talk loudly on the phone.

5.  **Work as a Team**

    - Share credit: Thank others for their help.

    - Help coworkers: Offer support when they need it.

    - Work together nicely: Team up respectfully for common goals.

**The Don'ts (Things to Avoid)**

1.  **Bad Communication and Behavior**

    - Don\'t talk loudly: Avoid loud phone calls or speakerphone in open
      areas.

    - Don\'t interrupt: Let people finish speaking.

    - Don\'t gossip: No rumors or negative talk about others.

    - Don\'t ignore messages: Reply to emails and chats.

    - Don\'t \"reply all\" unnecessarily: It fills inboxes.

    - Don\'t use bad language: Keep it professional.

    - Don\'t overshare: Avoid too many personal details.

2.  **Disrespecting Time and Space**

    - Don\'t be late often: It wastes others\' time.

    - Don\'t get distracted in meetings: No phones or side tasks.

    - Don\'t micromanage: Let people do their work.

    - Don\'t steal credit: Give credit where it\'s due.

3.  **Misusing Shared Areas**

    - Don\'t leave messes: Clean up after yourself.

    - Don\'t eat smelly food: Strong smells bother others.

    - Don\'t forget to mute: In online meetings, mute if there\'s noise.

4.  **Bad Personal Habits**

    - Don\'t skip hygiene: Stay clean and fresh.

    - Don\'t eat loudly: Chew quietly.
