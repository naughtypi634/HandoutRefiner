# Email Writing Practice

**Asking for a Clarification**
- Subject: Clarification needed on the project update
- Dear [Manager's name], I hope you're doing well.
- I wanted to follow up on the project update from yesterday's meeting.
- I'm unsure about [the deadline]. Could you clarify what is expected?
- Thank you! Best, [Your name]

**Requesting Time Off**
- Subject: Request for time off
- Dear [Supervisor's name], I hope you're doing well.
- I'd like to request a day off on [date] for a personal matter.
- I'll make sure all my tasks are up to date before I leave.
- Let me know if you need anything else. Sincerely, [Your name]

**Thanking a Colleague**
- Subject: Thank you for your support
- Hi [Colleague's name], I just wanted to say thank you.
- Your help with [the report] made a real difference to me.
- Looking forward to working together again! Best, [Your name]

**Following Up on a Task**
- Subject: Follow-up on the [report]
- Dear [Manager's name], I hope you're well.
- I wanted to confirm that I submitted the [report] as requested.
- Let me know if you need anything else from me. Best, [Your name]

**Requesting Feedback**
- Subject: Feedback on the [proposal]
- Dear [Supervisor's name], I hope you're having a good day.
- I've finished the [proposal] and would value your feedback.
- I want to make sure I'm meeting expectations. Best regards, [Your name]

**Networking with a Colleague**
- Subject: Coffee chat this week?
- Hi [Colleague's name], I hope you're doing well.
- I'd love to learn more about your work in [marketing].
- Would you be open to a coffee chat this week? Best, [Your name]

**Reporting a Workplace Issue**
- Subject: A safety concern on our floor
- Dear [Supervisor's name / HR], I hope you're well.
- I want to raise a safety issue I noticed near [the loading dock].
- [Briefly describe what happened.] Best regards, [Your name]

**Confirming a Meeting**
- Subject: Confirming our meeting
- Dear team, I'd like to confirm our meeting.
- We'll meet on [date] at [time] in [the meeting room].
- We'll discuss [the agenda]. Add any topics you'd like. Best, [Your name]
