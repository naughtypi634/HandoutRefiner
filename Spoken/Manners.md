# Manners

**Where Manners Matter**

- 餐厅与聚会 — dinner or a party：who sits down first, who eats first
- 会议 — meetings：arrive on time and do not interrupt
- 博物馆 — museums：do not touch what is on display
- 公共交通 — public transport：give up your seat when it is needed
- 插队 — cut in line：go in front of people who waited
- 图书馆 — libraries：keep your voice down
- 家庭聚会 — family gatherings：greet the older relatives first
- 线上聊天 — group chats：know when not to send a message
- 电影院 — movie theaters：no phones and no talking
- 洗手间 — public restrooms：leave it clean for the next person
- 打电话 — phone calls：know when it is okay to call

**Manners and Modern Life**

- 面子 — face：the respect you have in front of others
- 繁文缛节 — empty formalities：old rules that no longer mean much
- 假客气 — fake politeness：acting polite without meaning it
- 死板 — stiff：following rules with no warmth
- 代沟 — generational gap：old and young with different habits
- 社交距离 — personal space：the space you keep around your body
- 隐私 — privacy：the right to keep things to yourself

**Warm-up Discussion**

- What do you do to show politeness in everyday life?
- Why do you think we have manners in the first place?
- What are the table manners in your hometown?
- What should you bring or say when you visit someone's home?
- How much personal space do you need, and what do you do when someone stands too close?

**Discussion**

- On a crowded subway after a long day at work, an older person gets on. What do you do, and what goes through your mind?
- Someone's phone rings during a meeting. What do people around you usually do, and what do you do yourself?
- In a movie theater, the person in front of you films the screen with their phone. What do you do, if anything?
- You take a visiting friend out to dinner. Who pays, and how do you handle the bill without making it awkward?
- You are ten minutes late for a meeting. What do you do — message first, call, or just walk in?
- Traffic is heavy and another car wants to merge into your lane. Do you let it in, and what do most drivers in your city do?
- Someone asks you for a favor in a group chat and tags your name. How fast do you have to reply to stay polite?
- Which manners from the past have almost disappeared, and which new digital manners should exist?
- Who should teach children manners — parents, school, or society — and who taught you?
