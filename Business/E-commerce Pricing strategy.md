# E-commerce Pricing Strategy

**Pricing Strategies 定价策略**

- Value-based pricing — price set by what customers think it is worth
- Cost-plus pricing — the cost, plus a fixed markup on top
- Competitive pricing — match what other sellers are charging
- Economy pricing — the cheapest option, with no extras
- Penetration pricing — a very low price at launch to win users
- Dynamic pricing — the price changes with demand and the hour
- Price skimming — high at first, lower once rivals catch up
- Hourly pricing — the customer pays by the hour
- Project-based pricing — one price for the whole job
- High-low pricing — a normal price, with deep sale days
- Bundle pricing — several items together for less
- Geographic pricing — different prices in different places
- Psychological pricing — 9.9 instead of 10
- Freemium pricing — a free basic plan, paid upgrades
- Premium pricing — a high price to signal high quality
- Subscription pricing — pay by the month or the year

**Products 产品**

- Apps with VIP services · Luxury goods · Cars · Clothes
- Smartphones · Furniture · Fast food · Food chains
- Delivery · Phone cases · Toiletries · Airlines
- Ridesharing · Hotels · New medicines · High-end electronics
- Gym memberships
