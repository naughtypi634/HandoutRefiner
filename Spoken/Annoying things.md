# Annoying things

**\"It's the little things that drive us crazy!\"**

**Useful Expressions**

- **It drives me crazy when\...** (当......时我都快疯了)

- **I can\'t stand it when\...** (我最受不了......)

- **It's so annoying when\...** (......真是太让人烦了)

- **What really bugs me is\...** (真正让我烦恼的是......)

**On the Move (交通与出行)**

- **Traffic Jams:** Stuck in bumper-to-bumper traffic.

- **Public Transport:** Crowded buses/subways, missing the bus or train.

- **Ticket Troubles:** Sold out! Can\'t get the tickets you need.

- **The Elevator Wait:** Waiting forever, or stopping on every single
  floor.

**At Home (居家日常)**

- **The Mess:** Cluttered spaces, unorganized closets, and endless dust
  accumulation.

- **Things Breaking:** Clogged drains, broken household items.

- **Memory Lapses:** Misplaced items at home (Where are my keys?!).

**Tech & Communication (科技与通讯)**

- **Digital Distractions:** Constant spam calls and endless
  advertisements.

- **Tech Glitches:** Weak Wi-Fi/Internet problems, running on low
  battery, forgetting passwords.

- **Texting Traps:** Miscommunication via text, delayed responses from
  friends.

- **Overly Complicated Technology:** Gadgets that are too hard to use.

**Food & Shopping (饮食与购物)**

- **Dining Disasters:** Missing items in your takeout orders, difficult
  packaging that won\'t open.

- **Kitchen Nightmares:** Finding stale snacks or expired food in the
  fridge.

- **Shopping Stress:** Waiting in long lines at the checkout counter.

**Social & Out & About (社交与其它)**

- **Social Awkwardness:** Unwanted small talk, unexpected guests,
  unexpected changes in plans.

- **Daily Discomfort:** Wearing uncomfortable clothing/shoes, loud
  noises, sudden bad weather.
