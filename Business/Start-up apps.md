# Start-Up Apps

**Your Top Apps**

- List your five most-used apps. What category is each one in?
- Which app do you open first in the morning, and why?
- Which app do you use the longest every day? How long?
- Which of your apps do you pay for, and is it worth the money?
- Which app has been on your phone the longest?
- Did you delete any apps recently? Why?
- Which app would you struggle to live without?

**What Annoys You**

- What annoys you most when you use each of your top apps?
- What is the biggest flaw in the app you use most?
- Have you ever left a bad review? What made you do it?
- Which app has the worst ads, and how do they affect you?
- Which app keeps asking for permissions it doesn't really need?
- What app crashes or lags at the worst possible moment?

**Features**

- Which feature in your top app is completely unnecessary?
- What feature would you add to make the app better?
- What feature do you want improved, and how?
- Which app copies a feature from another app badly?
- What is one feature that made you switch to a new app?
- Is there a feature you never use but cannot turn off?
- What small change would save you time every single day?

**Designing an App**

- If you designed one app, would it be messaging, shopping, delivery, maps, travel, or social?
- What problem would your app solve, and who would use it?
- What is the hardest part to build — the idea, the money, or finding users?
- Would you build it alone or with a partner? Why?
- How would you test it before you launch?
- What would make users pick your app over the market leader?
- If a big company copied your idea, what would you do?

**Money and Users**

- How would your app make money — charging users or selling ads?
- Would you rather have many free users or a few paying ones?
- How much would you spend on ads to attract your first users?
- What makes you trust an app enough to pay for it?
- Have you ever paid for a subscription and forgotten to cancel it?
- Is it fair for apps to sell your data? Why or why not?

**Apps and Life**

- Which app has changed the way you live the most?
- How much time do you spend on your phone each day? Does it worry you?
- Do you check your phone during meals or before sleep? What stops you?
- Which app is the most useful for your work, and how do you use it?
- Do delivery and shopping apps save you time or just spend your money?
- What would a day without your phone look like?
