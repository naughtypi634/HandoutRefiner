# Consumerism

**Everyday Buying**

- What was the last thing you bought on your phone at midnight? Did you need it?
- You add something to your Taobao cart — how long do you wait before you pay?
- When you see a "today only, 50% off" banner, do you buy? What do you think?
- Do you buy cheap things and replace them often, or one good thing that lasts?
- What do you reorder in an app without even looking at the price?
- Walking past a shop window, what makes you stop and go inside?
- When you shop, do you make a list first, or decide as you go?
- What was the last thing you bought that you now use every single day?

**Livestream and Social Pressure**

- Do you watch livestream shopping? Have you ever bought from a streamer?
- A host shouts "only 100 left!" — do you believe it? Does it work on you?
- Have you ever bought something just because a friend already had it?
- Which app do you open first when you want to shop, and why that one?
- Who in your family spends the most, and what do they spend it on?
- Before you buy, do you read the reviews or just check the star rating?
- Do ads ever work on you? Tell us about the last ad you clicked.
- Have you ever bought something after an influencer posted about it?

**Money and Happiness**

- Does money buy happiness? Describe a purchase that made you very happy.
- What do you buy when you are stressed after a long week at work?
- What do the people around you buy to show off?
- What are you saving up for right now, and how is it going?
- Do you ever feel bad after spending a lot of money in one day?
- Is it better to spend on things or on experiences like travel and meals?
- Do the rich people around you seem happier than everyone else?
- What cheap little thing makes you happier than something expensive?

**Debt and Saving**

- Do you track your spending? How do you keep an eye on it?
- Do you pay in installments for anything? Is it helpful or risky?
- Have you ever lent money to a friend who never paid it back?
- How much of your monthly pay do you manage to save?
- Have you ever used a credit card or a "buy now, pay later" app?
- What is the most expensive thing you have ever paid for yourself?
- Do your parents think you save too little? Who do you think is right?
- If you suddenly got 10,000 yuan, what would you do with it?

**Regret and Returns**

- What is a purchase you regret? Tell us the whole story.
- What did you buy and then never use? Where is that thing now?
- Have you ever returned something? What happened at the counter?
- Do you care about famous brands? Give an example from your own closet.
- If one thing you own could be fully refunded, what would you pick?
- Have you ever hidden a purchase from your family? What happened?
- When do you realize a purchase was a mistake — right away or much later?
- What do you do with things you no longer want — sell, give, or store?
