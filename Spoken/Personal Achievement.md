The famous SMART model for setting a goal.

**Goal:** \"I want to read more books this year.\"

**SMART Breakdown:**

- **Specific:** I want to read 12 books this year, one book each month.

- **Measurable:** I will keep track of the books I read in a journal or
  an app.

- **Achievable:** I will set aside 30 minutes each day to read, which is
  realistic for my schedule.

- **Relevant:** Reading more books will help me relax and improve my
  knowledge on topics I'm interested in.

- **Time-bound:** I will complete my goal by December 31st of this year.

So the complete SMART goal would be: \"I will read one book each month
for a total of 12 books by December 31st, dedicating 30 minutes each day
to reading.\"
