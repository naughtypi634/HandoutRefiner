# Personal Achievement

**Achievements So Far**

- What is your biggest achievement so far?
- What was your most recent one, and how did you make it happen?
- Who did you tell when it happened?
- Do you celebrate your wins, or do you move straight on to the next thing?
- Do you post your achievements anywhere, or keep them to yourself?
- Are you proud of something nobody else knows about?

**What Made It Happen**

- How much of success is hard work, and how much is being in the right place at the right time?
- Who helped you get where you are today?
- What is something you worked hard for and still did not get?
- Have you ever gotten lucky with something you did not work for?
- Which habit has helped you the most?
- Do you work better with a deadline, or when nobody is watching?

**Goals That Work**

- What is one goal you have right now?
- Can you say it in one clear sentence?
- How would you know you actually did it? What would you count?
- Is it doable with your real schedule, or is it a wish?
- Why does this goal matter to you?
- When do you want it done, exactly?

**Failing and Trying Again**

- Have you ever failed to reach a goal? What happened?
- What did that failure teach you?
- When a plan is not working, do you change the plan or drop the goal?
- Do you quit early or push through? Give an example.
- What would you say to a friend who just failed at something?
- Is one failure enough to stop trying?

**What Achievement Means**

- In your own words, what is achievement?
- What does your family or culture expect you to achieve by thirty?
- Is success mostly about money, or something else?
- Which common life goals do you actually want: a house, marriage, a steady job, or something else?
- Do you compare your achievements with your friends'? How does that feel?
- Is it better to enjoy the process or to hit the goal?
- What do you want to be able to say you have done in ten years?
- What would you try if you knew you could not fail?
