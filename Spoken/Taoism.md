- **Here are some of the famous quotes. What do they mean? What are your
  thoughts?**

  - The journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step.

  - When you are content to be simply yourself and don't compare or
    compete, everyone will respect you.

  - Nature does not hurry, yet everything is accomplished.

  - Knowing others is intelligence; knowing yourself is true wisdom.
    Mastering others is strength; mastering yourself is true power.

  - A good traveler has no fixed plans and is not intent on arriving.

  - He who knows that enough is enough will always have enough.

  - The best way to hold onto something is to let it go.

  - To know the Tao is to know the way of nature.

  - The flame that burns twice as bright burns half as long.

  - Happiness is the absence of the striving for happiness.
