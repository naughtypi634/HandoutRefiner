# Making Friends on Social Media

**Who You Add**

- How many of your WeChat friends have you actually met in person?
- Do you accept friend requests from people you have never met?
- What makes you follow someone — their posts, photos, or humor?
- How do you decide an online stranger is worth chatting with?
- Have you ever removed or blocked a friend? What happened?
- Do you keep online friends separate from your real ones?
- Do you ever add someone you met once at a party or a meeting?

**Real or Just Good-Looking**

- When you post, do you share real life or only the nice parts?
- Do you post only photos you have edited and filtered? Why?
- How do you feel when your post gets very few likes?
- Have you ever copied a friend's post or a trend you saw?
- How honest are people about their lives on Moments or short video?
- Can you tell when someone online is faking their life?
- Do you compare your life with your friends' feeds? How does it feel?

**Making a Real Connection**

- What is the best way to start a message to a new online friend?
- Do you prefer chatting by text, by voice message, or by video?
- How long before chatting starts to feel like a real friendship?
- What do you talk about with someone you only know online?
- Do you remember your online friends' birthdays or big days?
- What makes an online friend different from an old pen pal?
- Have you ever sent a gift or helped a friend you met online?

**Staying Safe**

- Have you ever heard of someone being tricked by an online "friend"?
- What tricks do scammers use to win your trust?
- Would you share your address or ID with someone you met online?
- What do you do if a stranger online asks you for money?
- How do you check whether a new online friend is real?
- What is one safety rule you never break online?
- Should apps warn you when a stranger seems suspicious?

**From Screens to Real Life**

- Have you ever met an online friend in person? How did it go?
- Where would you meet someone for the first time — a café or a mall?
- Is it easier to make friends online or face to face? Why?
- Can an online friendship be as strong as an offline one?
- What is the best thing you have gotten from an online friendship?
- Does social media help you feel less lonely, or more?

**Friendships That Fade**

- Why do some online friendships quietly stop one day?
- Do you ever feel tired of keeping up with too many friends?
- Have you ever lost a friend because of something posted online?
- Is it okay to unfollow a friend without telling them?
- How do you keep a friendship going when you are both busy?
- What makes a friendship worth the effort to keep?
