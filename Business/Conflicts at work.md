your thoughts.**

do?

If your manager assigns you an unrealistic deadline, how would you
handle it?

If a team member consistently misses deadlines, or is sluggish, how
would you address the issue?

If you're paired with someone with a drastically different work style,
what would you do?

If you witness two colleagues in a heated argument, would you be the
peacemaker?

If you feel undervalued compared to peers, how would you discuss this
with leadership?

If you're asked to perform tasks outside your role, how would you
respond?

If you suspect favoritism is affecting promotions, what would you do?

If work pressure leads to burnout, how would you address it with your
manager?

If cultural differences cause tension in your team, how would you foster
understanding?
