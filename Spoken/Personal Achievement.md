# Personal Achievement

**Wins So Far**

- What was your first job? How old were you?
- Have you ever passed a really hard exam? Which one?
- What is something you learned all by yourself?
- Have you ever fixed something with your own hands?
- Have you ever finished something long, like a course or a big project?
- What is something you did that made your parents proud?
- What are you good at that your friends are not?

**Work and Money**

- Have you ever gotten a better job or a raise? How did you do it?
- Have you ever saved up for something expensive? What was it?
- Have you ever paid off a big bill, like a phone or a laptop?
- Have you ever passed a job interview you were nervous about?
- Do you have a side job or a small business? How is it going?
- What is the hardest part of your week?
- Have you ever gotten a certificate? Was it worth the money?

**Small Goals**

- What is one thing you want to finish this week?
- What do you want to buy next? How much is it?
- Do you want to sleep more, move more, or eat better?
- How many hours a week do you want to study English?
- What time do you want to get up tomorrow? Will you?
- What is one small habit you want to start this month?
- How would you know you did it? What would you count?

**Failing and Trying Again**

- Have you ever quit something? What was it?
- Have you ever failed a test or an interview? What did you do next?
- Do you give up fast, or do you keep trying? Give an example.
- When a plan does not work, what do you change?
- Have you ever tried the same thing three times? What was it?
- What would you say to a friend who just failed?

**Talking About It**

- Do you tell people when something good happens?
- Do you post good news online, or keep it quiet?
- How do you celebrate: a dinner, a gift, or a day off?
- Do you feel shy when people praise you?
- Does your family talk about achievements? Whose?
- Do you compare yourself with your friends? How does it feel?

**Bigger Picture**

- What does success mean to you: money, family, health, or something else?
- Do you think luck matters? Give one example from your life.
- Who has helped you the most so far?
- In ten years, what do you want your life to look like?
- What is one thing you want to do before you are 40?
- What is one thing you want to do this year, no matter what?
- What would you try if you were not afraid to fail?
