**Challenge**

- Find all the colors within sight

**Discussion**

- What's your favorite color? Has it changed over the years?
- When you buy clothes, what colors do you usually go for — light, dark, or bright?
- What color are the shoes you wear most, and why that color?
- What colors do you dislike? Why?
- What colors do you see around you right now? Is there one you'd never use at home?
- If you were buying a car, what color would you get? What color would you never get?
- How would you describe the color of your phone case — closer to navy, olive, or beige?
- Are you good at matching colors? Which two colors look good together, and which two clash?
- When you decorate a place, do you go light and airy, or dark and heavy?
- Which colors do you wear when you want to look serious at work?
- What's the most popular color in your city? What color are most of the buildings?
- Do colors affect your mood? Give an example from this week.
- What kind of situation makes you see red? What about going red in the face?
- What kind of week makes you feel blue, and what do you do about it?
- Do colors play a part in the brands you like? What color do you think of first for your favorite brand?
- Is there a color you used to hate but like now? What changed?
- If your personality were a color, what would it be, and why?
- Some people think pink is a feminine color. How do you feel about men wearing pink?
- Have you ever dyed your hair a different color? What made you change it, and what color did you go with?
- Do you do anything to make your skin lighter or darker? What do you use?
- Have you ever met someone who's colorblind? Which colors couldn't they see?
