# Conflicts at Work

**Handling Conflict**

- What is the biggest conflict you have had at work? How did you handle it?
- How do you feel when a conflict starts? Do you stay calm or get nervous?
- Do you deal with a problem at once, or wait until you cool down?
- Have you ever made a conflict worse by saying the wrong thing?
- What could you do better the next time a conflict comes up?
- Do you talk to the person directly, or go to your manager first?
- Is it ever better to just let a small problem go? When?

**What Would You Do?**

- A colleague takes credit for your idea in a meeting. What do you do?
- Your manager sets a deadline you know is impossible. How do you respond?
- A teammate keeps missing deadlines. How do you bring it up?
- You are paired with someone whose work style is the opposite of yours?
- Two colleagues are shouting at each other in a meeting. Do you step in?
- You feel underpaid next to your peers. How do you raise it with your boss?
- Your boss asks you to do work outside your job description. What now?
- You suspect the boss's favourite always gets the promotion. What do you do?
- Work pressure is burning you out. How do you tell your manager?
- Cultural differences are causing tension in your team. How do you fix it?

**When It Gets Personal**

- A colleague says something rude about your work in front of others. Now what?
- Someone keeps interrupting you in every meeting. How do you handle it?
- A teammate never replies to your messages. How do you get an answer?
- Your boss criticises you in front of the whole team. What do you say?
- A friend at work is angry that you got the project, not them. What do you do?
- Two teammates are not speaking, and you are in the middle. What now?
- A colleague shares your private chat with the team. What do you do?

**Keeping the Peace**

- How do you disagree with your boss without sounding rude?
- What is the best way to give someone bad news about their work?
- How do you say "no" to a colleague without hurting the friendship?
- Do you apologise when you are wrong, even if it feels awkward?
- How can a team argue well and still stay friendly the next day?
- Is it ever fine to go over your manager's head? When?
- What does a good apology sound like at work?

**Lessons Learned**

- What conflict have you learned the most from? What happened?
- Do you argue more with bosses or with people at your own level? Why?
- Is a team with no conflict a good team, or a silent one?
- How does culture change what counts as rude at work?
- Have you ever seen a small argument turn into a big problem?
- What would you do differently in your last conflict?
- What is the one rule you follow in every conflict?

**Your Own Examples**

- What would be a real, serious conflict for you? Why?
- Can you write down three conflicts you can easily let go of?
