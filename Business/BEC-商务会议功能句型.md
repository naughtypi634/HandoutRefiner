# 商务会议功能句型

**Getting the Chairperson's Attention 引起主席注意**

- Mr./Madam Chairman.
- May I have a word?
- If I may, I think...
- Excuse me for interrupting.
- May I come in here?

**Giving Opinions 表达意见**

- I'm positive that...
- I really feel that...
- In my opinion...
- The way I see things...
- If you ask me, I tend to think...

**Asking for Opinions 询问意见**

- Are you positive that...?
- Do you really think that...?
- Can we get your input?
- How do you feel about...?

**Commenting 做出评论**

- That's interesting.
- I never thought of it that way.
- Good point!
- I get your point.
- I see what you mean.

**Agreeing 表示同意**

- I totally agree with you.
- Exactly!
- That's the way I feel.
- I have to agree with you.

**Disagreeing 表示异议**

- Unfortunately, I see it differently.
- Up to a point I agree, but...
- I'm afraid I can't agree.

**Advising and Suggesting 提出建议**

- Let's...
- We should...
- Why don't you...?
- How about...?
- I suggest that...

**Clarifying 澄清**

- Let me spell it out...
- Have I made that clear?
- Do you see what I mean?
- Let me put this another way...
- I'd just like to repeat that...

**Requesting Information 请求信息**

- Could you please...?
- I'd like you to...
- Would you mind...?
- I wonder if you could...

**Asking for Repetition 请求重复**

- Sorry, I didn't catch that.
- Could you repeat that, please?
- Could you say that again?
- Could you run that by me again?

**Asking for Clarification 要求澄清**

- I don't quite follow.
- What exactly do you mean?
- I'm not sure I understand.
- Could you explain how that works?
- Could we have some more details?

**Asking for Verification 请求确认**

- You did say next week, didn't you?
- Do you mean that...?
- Is it true that...?

**Asking for Spelling 请求拼写**

- Could you spell that, please?
- Would you mind spelling that for me?

**Asking for Contributions 请求赐教**

- We haven't heard from you yet.
- What do you think about this?
- Would you like to add anything?
- Anyone else got anything to add?
- Any more comments?

**Correcting Information 更正**

- Sorry, I think you misunderstood.
- Sorry, that's not quite right.
- That's not what I meant.

**Keeping the Meeting on Target 转入正题**

- We're running short of time.
- Let's be brief.
- I'm afraid we've run out of time.
- That's outside the scope here.
- Let's get back on track.
- That's not why we're here today.
- Let's return to the main focus.
- We'll leave that for another time.
- Keep to the point, please.
- Are we ready to make a decision?
