**What gives you stress at work?**

- 工作量过大 — Excessive workloads：too much work to do in a day
- 截止时间紧张 — Tight deadlines：not enough time to finish your work
- 薪资低 — Low salary：the money you get is not enough
- 职业天花板 — Glass ceiling：no chance of getting a better job
- 缺乏自主权 — Lack of control：you cannot decide things for yourself
- 工作与生活失衡 — Poor work-life balance：no time for family or your own life
- 人际冲突 — Interpersonal conflicts：problems with people at work
- 工作不安全感 — Job insecurity：you are afraid of losing your job
- 重复性工作 — Repetitive work：doing the same thing every single day

**What happens when you feel stressed?**

- 头痛 — Headaches：pain in your head
- 疲劳 — Fatigue：feeling very tired
- 焦虑与抑郁 — Anxiety and depression：worried or sad all the time
- 睡眠障碍 — Sleep problems：sleeping badly
- 失眠 — Insomnia：not being able to fall asleep
- 易怒 — Irritability：getting angry easily about small things
- 消化问题 — Digestive issues：stomach problems
- 高血压 — High blood pressure：blood pressure too high
- 食欲改变 — Changes in appetite：eating more or less than usual
- 重复性劳损 — RSI (repetitive strain injury)：pain in arms or hands from doing the same work
- 肥胖 — Obesity：gaining too much weight
- 压力性进食 — Stress eating：eating a lot to calm down

**How do you cope with stress?**

- 时间管理 — Time management：planning your time well
- 休息与放松 — Breaks and downtime：taking short breaks
- 运动 — Exercise：working out or going to the gym
- 饮食 — Diet：eating healthy food
- 冥想 — Meditation：sitting quietly to relax
- 休假 — Taking a holiday：getting away from work for a while
- 旅行 — Travel：going on a trip

**Discussion**

- People in your team chat still send messages at 10 pm, even after
  work finishes. How does that make you feel, and what do you do so
  your phone doesn't stress you out at night?

- After a hard day, how do you relax on the way home — music on the
  metro, a walk, or your favorite takeout? Tell us what you really do.

- When you feel stressed at work, what is the first thing you usually
  do to feel better? Try to describe a real moment.

- Mondays can be hard. What is the worst part of Monday morning for
  you, and what helps you get up and go to work?

- Your boss sends you another task just as you're about to leave at
  6 pm. What do you say to them, and how do you feel inside?
