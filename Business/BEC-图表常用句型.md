# 图表常用句型

## 上升 Upward

**小幅上升**

- go up — 上升 — Delivery orders go up 5% every Friday.
- rise — 上涨 — Rent rose 8% in one year.
- increase — 增长 — Revenue increased by 12% last quarter.
- gradually — 逐渐 — Prices gradually climbed all year.
- be on the rise — 持续上升 — Rents here are on the rise again.
- edge up — 小幅上升 — Gas prices edged up 2% this week.
- a little — 稍微 — Sales went up a little in May.
- slightly — 略微 — Costs rose slightly, about 1%.

**稳步上升**

- grow — 增长 — Online sales grew 20% in a year.
- climb — 攀升 — Coffee prices climbed to $40 a bag.
- steadily — 稳步 — Demand grew steadily at 2% a month.
- climb steadily — 稳步攀升 — Coffee sales climbed steadily for 3 years.
- trend upward — 呈上升趋势 — Sales have trended upward since March.
- gather pace — 增速加快 — Hiring gathered pace in the spring.
- noticeably — 明显 — Traffic increased noticeably after the ad.
- post double-digit growth — 实现两位数增长 — The unit posted double-digit growth.

**大幅上升**

- jump — 激增 — Tickets jumped from $28 to $79.
- leap — 跃升 — Sales leapt 30% in launch week.
- surge — 猛增 — Orders surged 40% in one month.
- soar — 飙涨 — Airfares soared 60% before the holiday.
- skyrocket — 暴涨 — Delivery fees skyrocketed overnight.
- more than double — 翻了一倍多 — Membership more than doubled in 18 months.
- go through the roof — 涨到离谱 — Rent near the subway went through the roof.
- considerably — 相当大 — Rents rose considerably, close to 15%.
- sharply — 急剧 — The rate rose sharply in one week.
- steeply — 陡峭 — Energy costs climbed steeply in June.
- dramatically — 大幅 — Ad spend rose dramatically last quarter.

**见顶** <!-- shape=top_out -->

- hit a high — 达到高点 — Bookings hit a high in June.
- peak — 达到峰值 — App traffic peaks at 9 p.m.
- top out — 见顶 — Apartment prices topped out in June.
- hit a record high — 创历史新高 — Exports hit a record high in Q2.
- surge to a record high — 飙升至新高 — Exports surged to a record high in Q2.

## 下降 Downward

**小幅下降**

- go down — 下降 — Downloads went down 3% last month.
- fall — 下跌 — Coffee prices fell 10% this year.
- decrease — 减少 — Office visits decreased by 15%.
- dip — 短暂下跌 — Sales dipped in February.
- be on the decline — 持续下降 — Print ads are on the decline.
- edge down — 小幅下降 — Grocery prices edged down 1%.
- marginally — 略微 — Sales dipped marginally in March.
- modestly — 小幅 — Fees fell modestly, about 4%.

**稳步下滑**

- drop — 下降 — Heating costs dropped in April.
- decline — 下滑 — Book sales declined for two years.
- slide — 持续下滑 — The stock slid for a third straight week.
- gently — 平缓 — The curve declined gently all year.
- steadily — 持续 — Costs dropped steadily for six months.
- fall steadily — 持续下跌 — Enrollment fell steadily since 2019.
- trend downward — 呈下降趋势 — Screen time trended downward this year.
- lose ground — 失去份额 — The brand lost ground to cheaper rivals.

**大幅下跌**

- slump — 大幅下跌 — Imports slumped 20% in one quarter.
- plunge — 暴跌 — Ticket prices plunged after the holiday.
- plummet — 急剧暴跌 — Oil demand plummeted in early 2026.
- crash — 崩盘 — Crypto prices crashed in one weekend.
- take a nosedive — 急剧下滑 — Sales took a nosedive in the second half.
- fall off a cliff — 断崖式下跌 — Orders fell off a cliff after the promo.
- shrink by half — 减少一半 — The night team shrank by half in a year.
- by a wide margin — 以很大幅度 — Orders missed the target by a wide margin.
- sharply — 急剧 — Hotel bookings dropped sharply in August.
- dramatically — 大幅 — Rents fell dramatically after the new rules.

**触底** <!-- shape=bottom_out -->

- bottom out — 触底 — Orders bottomed out in May.
- reach a low point — 达到低点 — Team morale reached a low point in March.
- hit a record low — 创历史新低 — The rate hit a record low in May.
- plunge to a record low — 跌至历史低点 — The currency plunged to a record low.

## 波动 Fluctuation

**小幅波动**

- vary — 变化不定 — Delivery times vary from week to week.
- fluctuate — 波动 — Gas prices fluctuated all summer.
- move up and down — 上下波动 — Traffic moves up and down with the school year.
- range between A and B — 在……区间波动 — Sales range between 8,000 and 12,000 units.
- stay within a narrow range — 窄幅波动 — The index stayed within a narrow range.

**来回摆动**

- wobble — 小幅震荡 — The exchange rate wobbled after the news.
- swing — 大幅摆动 — Customer sentiment swung week to week.
- bounce around — 来回跳动 — Weekend prices bounced around all month.
- swing back and forth — 来回摆动 — The number swung back and forth all year.

**反复涨跌**

- seesaw — 反复涨跌 — Rates seesawed in the second half.
- oscillate — 来回震荡 — Demand oscillated around 1,000 units.
- turn volatile — 变得不稳定 — The market turned volatile in April.

**剧烈震荡**

- fluctuate wildly — 剧烈波动 — Crypto prices fluctuated wildly in March.
- show wild swings — 出现大幅波动 — The index showed wild swings in June.
- be all over the place — 毫无规律 — Weekly numbers were all over the place.

## 平稳 Flat / Steady

**保持不变**

- stay — 保持 — Prices stayed around $2 all week.
- hold — 维持 — Demand held at the same level.
- remain — 保持 — The rate remained at 3%.
- stay flat — 保持不变 — Revenue stayed flat in Q3.
- remain stable — 保持稳定 — Prices remained stable all year.
- hold steady — 保持平稳 — Wage growth held steady at 3%.
- show little change — 变化不大 — Foot traffic showed little change.

**趋于平稳** <!-- shape=plateau -->

- stabilize — 趋于稳定 — Supply stabilized after the strike.
- plateau — 进入平台期 — Subscriptions plateaued in June.
- level off — 趋于平稳 — Sales leveled off in the fall.
- flatten — 曲线走平 — The curve flattened after 6 p.m.
- level off at — 稳定在 — Sales leveled off at 20,000 units.
- plateau at — 停滞在 — Growth plateaued at 4% this quarter.
