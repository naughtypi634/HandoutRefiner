**Discussion**

- Do you know your MBTI type, your blood type, and your zodiac sign?
- Do you agree with your MBTI result? Which part fits you, and which does not?
- How much do you believe the blood-type chart? Why?
- When do you find yourself checking your horoscope the most?
- Why are MBTI and zodiac signs so popular with young people now?
- Do your friends match their star sign, or is it just fun?
- Do you use MBTI to choose friends or partners? Be honest.
- Have you ever changed your mind about someone because of their sign?
- Are these systems simple fun, or unfair labels? What do you think?
- Can a twelve-row chart really describe a whole person?
- Do you and your coworkers talk about MBTI at work?
- Would you ever put your MBTI type on a job application?
- Which feels more accurate for you — MBTI, blood type, or zodiac?
- Do you read your weekly horoscope? Do you believe it?
- Why do people like putting themselves and others into types?
- Is it unfair to judge someone by their sign before you know them?
- What personality test would you design to describe yourself?
- If these charts are not real science, why do they still feel true?
- Would you date someone whose sign or type you dislike?
- Do people use "it's my sign" as an excuse for bad behavior?
