# Love is a Mystery

**Love and Crushes**

- Do you believe in love at first sight? Have you ever felt it?
- What do you consider really romantic — a gift, or a homemade meal?
- What is your idea of a perfect date?
- Who asked whom out on your first date? How did it happen?
- Do you believe in soulmates? Why or why not?
- How does a "like" online turn into a real crush?
- Have you ever liked someone your friends did not approve of?

**What Matters in a Partner**

- What is the most important quality in a romantic partner?
- How important is physical attractiveness to you? Be honest.
- Do partners need to share hobbies and interests? Why or why not?
- Does age matter in a relationship? What about height or income?
- Would you date someone your parents did not like?
- Is friendship or romantic love more important to you?
- What would you never accept in a partner?

**Staying Together**

- How do you handle a disagreement with your partner?
- What is the best relationship advice you have ever heard?
- What do you do to keep a long-term relationship fresh?
- Is it okay to keep some things private from your partner?
- Who should pay on a date — the man, the woman, or split it?
- What small habits keep a couple happy over the years?
- What makes a couple break up after years together?

**Distance and Timing**

- Can long-distance relationships work? Have you tried one?
- How do you stay close to a partner who lives in another city?
- Is it better to be single or in a relationship? Why?
- Should people marry young, or wait until later? Why?
- What do you think of blind dates set up by family?
- Does a busy job ever get in the way of a relationship?
- Would you move cities for the person you love?

**Online Dating**

- What are the good and bad points of dating apps?
- Which dating apps are popular in China right now?
- How do you know if someone is lying in their profile?
- Is it safe to meet an online date in person? How do you stay safe?
- Have you ever heard of a dating scam? What happened?
- Has online dating made love easier or more complicated?
- Could you fall in love with someone you only met online?

**When Love Ends**

- How do you get over a breakup? What helps the most?
- Are you still friends with any of your exes?
- What is a red flag you would not ignore again?
- Is it ever worth getting back together with an ex?
- How do you tell someone you are not interested anymore?
- What did your last relationship teach you about yourself?
