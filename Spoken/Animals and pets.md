# Animals and pets

**Challenge**

- List as many animals as you can in two minutes with your partner.

**Appearance 外观**

- cute — 可爱的
- pretty — 好看的
- fluffy — 毛茸茸的
- smooth — 光滑的
- tiny — 小巧的
- chubby — 圆滚滚的
- scruffy — 乱蓬蓬的
- well-groomed — 打理得干干净净的
- purebred — 纯种的

**Personality 性格**

- friendly — 友好的
- gentle — 温柔的
- smart — 聪明的
- loyal — 忠诚的
- shy — 怕生的
- stubborn — 倔强的
- calm — 沉稳的
- easygoing — 温顺好相处的
- patient — 有耐心的

**Behavior 行为**

- clingy — 粘人的
- naughty — 调皮的
- playful — 活泼好动的
- curious — 好奇的
- noisy — 爱叫的、吵的
- house-trained — 会定点上厕所的
- independent — 独来独往的
- well-behaved — 守规矩的
- aggressive — 凶的、爱咬人的

**Daily Care 日常照顾**

- walk the dog — 遛狗
- feed the cat — 喂猫
- litter box — 猫砂盆
- cat litter — 猫砂
- scratching post — 猫抓板
- leash — 牵引绳
- pet carrier — 宠物航空箱
- keep it on a leash — 牵好狗绳
- pick up after it — 捡便便
- grooming — 洗澡美容
- pet food — 宠物粮
- monthly cost — 每月开销

**At the Vet 宠物医院**

- vaccination — 打疫苗
- deworming — 驱虫
- checkup — 体检
- get spayed — 做绝育
- microchip — 植入芯片
- flea and tick — 跳蚤和蜱虫
- pet insurance — 宠物保险
- boarding — 寄养
- pet travel permit — 宠物托运证明
- vet bill — 看病的账单

**Discussion**

- Do you have a pet? Tell your partner about it.
- How much does a pet cost each month, food and vet bills included?
- What would you do with your pet if you had to travel for a month?
- Would you rather adopt a stray or buy one from a pet store?
- Why do people watch cat videos when they don't even have a cat?
- Have you ever seen someone walk a dog without a leash?
- What are the rules about dogs in your neighborhood?
- How much should people spend on pet care?
- Would you let your pet sleep on your bed? Why or why not?
- Should pet owners treat their pets like children?
- What do you think of pets wearing clothes or having dyed fur?
- Have you ever taken a pet to the vet? What happened?
- Do you know anyone who brings a pet on the subway or a train?
- How should cities deal with stray cats and dogs?
- Are zoos good or cruel for animals?
- What is your favorite animal, and why?
- Which animals make the best pets? Why?
- If you could be any animal, which one would you be?
- Would an animal-welfare label change what you buy?
- Some people let their dog lick their face. What do you think?
- What are your responsibilities if you keep a pet?
- Why are some animals endangered, and what can we do?
- Would you keep an unusual pet, like a reptile or a bird?
- What makes humans different from animals?
