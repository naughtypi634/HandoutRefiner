# Ultimate Travel Packing List

**Carry-On vs Checked**

- Sharp items: scissors, razor blades, knives.
- Flammable items: lighters, matches, aerosols.
- Potential weapons: bats, clubs, hammers.
- Liquids over 100 ml.
- Some food and drinks.

**Day-to-Day Clothes**

- Underwear
- Socks
- T-shirts
- Jackets
- Jumpers
- Jeans
- Shorts
- Pants
- Belt
- Dresses
- Skirts
- Comfortable shoes
- Pyjamas
- Hat
- Glasses and case

**Beach Clothes**

- Swimwear
- Sun hat
- Flip-flops or sandals
- Beach towel
- Wet bag
- Sunscreen

**Cold Weather Clothes**

- Coat
- Windbreaker
- Scarf
- Gloves
- Thermals
- Travel umbrella
- Rain jacket
- Boots
- Hand-warmers

**Adventure Clothes**

- Hiking shoes
- Loose-fitting long-sleeved shirt
- Mosquito net
- Quick-drying shirt and shorts
- Waterproof socks
- Water bottle

**Dining Out Clothes**

- Dress pants
- Belt
- Dress shoes
- Formal shirt
- Formal dress
- Coat
- Purse or wallet
- Handbag
- Jewellery (pack in carry-on)

**Toiletries**

- Toothbrush and toothpaste
- Floss
- Shampoo and conditioner
- Body wash
- Lip balm
- Make-up and cosmetics
- Perfume
- Cleanser, toner, moisturizer
- Cotton buds
- Contact lenses and solution
- Hair styling products
- Nail clippers, tweezers, scissors
- Laundry kit

**Health and Medicine**

- Prescription medicine
- Cold and flu medicine
- Pain relief medicine
- Diarrhea medicine
- Motion sickness tablets
- First aid kit
- Sunburn gel (aloe vera)
- Vitamins
- Insect repellent
- Eye drops
- Hand sanitizer

**Electronics**

- Power adapter for your destination
- Mobile phone and charger
- Laptop or tablet and charger
- Camera, memory card, charger
- Protective cases
- Noise cancelling headphones
- eReader and charger
- GoPro
- Translation and map apps

**Travel Documents**

- Passport and photocopies
- Local currency and credit cards
- Driver's license
- Travel insurance documents
- Visas
- Booking confirmations and itineraries
- International SIM card or eSIM
- Emergency and embassy contacts

**Safety and Security**

- Luggage lock
- Money belt

**Discussion**

- Pack light, or bring everything?
- How do you pack for hot or cold trips?
- Do you use a packing list?
- How many packed clothes do you wear?
- What can't you travel without?
- What's the strangest thing you packed?
- Books and games, or just your phone?
- Ever forgotten something crucial?
- What's your biggest packing mistake?
- Five things for a month: which ones?
- Beach trip vs city break?
- What do you pack for a long flight?
