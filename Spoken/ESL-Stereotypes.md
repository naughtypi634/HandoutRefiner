Stereotypes

**What Are Stereotypes?**

Stereotypes are oversimplified beliefs or assumptions about a group of
people based on characteristics like gender, age, occupation, or region.
They often ignore individual differences and can lead to unfair
judgments.

**Where did we get the stereotypes?**

- **Media**: TV, movies, and social media often reinforce stereotypes
  (e.g., portraying scientists as nerdy).

- **School education**: Textbooks may oversimplify cultures.

- **Social Interactions**: Family and peers can reinforce stereotypes.

- **Cognitive Bias**: Humans naturally categorize to simplify the world.
