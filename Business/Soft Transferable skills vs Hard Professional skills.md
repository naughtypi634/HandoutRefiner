# Soft Transferable Skills vs Hard Professional Skills

**Warm-Up**
- What is your strongest skill at work?
- List as many soft skills as you can. Now list hard, job-specific skills.
- Where do you draw the line between the two?

**Common Soft Skills**
- Communication · teamwork · problem-solving · time management
- Adaptability · leadership · critical thinking · interpersonal skills
- Organization · emotional intelligence · creativity · negotiation

**Common Hard Skills**
- Excel and data analysis · coding · a second language
- Accounting · design software · operating machinery
- Copywriting · project management tools · a driving licence

**Discussion**
- For each skill above: what does it mean, and how does it shape your day?
- Which transferable skills should every professional have?
- Which type matters more in general? And in your own industry?
- Can a hard skill be learned faster than a soft skill? Give an example.
- How much time do you spend improving your soft and hard skills?
- How could your leader or team help you build these skills?
- How do you improve these skills on your own, after work?
- What tools or apps do you use to practise them?
- Which soft skill is hardest to fake in an interview, and why?
- If you had to hire for one and train the other, which would you pick?
