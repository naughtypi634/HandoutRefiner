# New Year's Resolution

**Last Year**

- Was the last year a good year for you? What were its highlights?
- What was not good about last year for you?
- Did you achieve any of your goals last year?
- Did you keep the resolutions you made at the start of the year?
- What was your biggest surprise last year?
- What was the best day you had last year? What happened?
- What do you wish you had done differently last year?

**Celebrations**

- How do you usually celebrate the New Year?
- Is it a family dinner, a party with friends, or something else?
- Why don't you watch the Spring Festival Gala? Or do you?
- Where do you go for the New Year's Eve dinner?
- Do you exchange gifts with family or friends? What do you give?
- Do you know any special main dish for New Year's Eve?
- What are some New Year traditions in your hometown?

**The Spring Festival**

- How many days off do you have for the Spring Festival? What's your plan?
- What do you buy for the Spring Festival?
- Do you travel during the holiday, or stay home? Why?
- How do you get your tickets home? How early do you book?
- Do you give or receive red packets? How does it work in your family?
- What is your favorite part of the Spring Festival? Why?
- What part of the holiday do you find most tiring?

**Resolutions and Goals**

- Will you make any New Year's resolutions? What are they?
- What do you intend to do differently next year?
- What is one habit you want to start in the new year?
- What is one thing you want to stop doing?
- Have you ever written your resolutions down? Did that help?
- How long do your resolutions usually last? Be honest.
- What is a goal you set for 2026? How will you reach it?

**Looking Ahead**

- What are your plans for 2026?
- What do you hope will change in your life this year?
- Do you have a good feeling about the future?
- Are you optimistic or pessimistic about the next few years? Why?
- What is one thing you want to learn in the new year?
- Who do you want to spend more time with this year?
- If this year goes perfectly, what will be different by next December?

**New Year Around You**

- How do your parents celebrate the New Year differently from you?
- What New Year food does your family always make?
- Do you clean the house before the New Year? Who does it?
- What is the busiest thing in your city during the holiday?
- How do you spend your last day before going back to work?
