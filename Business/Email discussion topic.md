# Email Basics

**Your Account**

- Which email provider do you use? Is one better than another?
- Have you ever paid for extra storage or premium email services?
- Do you know how to set up an email account on your phone?
- How many email accounts do you have? What is each one for?
- When did you create your first email account? How old were you?
- Have you ever forgotten a password and lost an old account?
- Would you use a company email address for personal things? Why?

**Providers**

- Is Gmail, Outlook, or QQ Mail better for work? Why?
- Do you care about privacy when you choose an email provider?
- Have you ever changed email providers? What made you move?
- Is a school or work email ever worth keeping after you leave?
- Do you trust free email, or would you pay for a safer one?
- What do you think of email addresses full of random numbers?

**Everyday Email**

- How many emails do you get in a day? How many are spam?
- Do you use email for personal things, or only for work?
- What do you use email for most — work, shopping, or sign-ups?
- Do you still send personal emails to friends, or only WeChat?
- How much time do you need to write a proper email?
- Do you check email before bed or first thing in the morning?
- Which emails do you never open? Why?

**Spam and Safety**

- How much spam do you get every week? What is it about?
- Have you ever clicked a link in a suspicious email? What happened?
- How can you tell a real email from a scam? Give an example.
- Do you report spam, or just delete it?
- Have you ever been tricked by a fake delivery or bank email?
- What should you do if you get an email asking for money?
- Do you think email will ever be completely safe from scams?

**History and Change**

- Do you remember the first email you ever sent? When was it?
- How did people share news before email? Do you remember?
- Has email made you more organized, or more stressed?
- Why is email still so popular at work when WeChat is faster?
- Do young people today use email less than their parents did?
- Will email still exist in twenty years? What might replace it?
- What would make you finally close an old email account?

**Email in China**

- Do most people in China use email or WeChat for work? Why?
- Is email more common in foreign companies than in Chinese ones?
- Do you write work emails in English, Chinese, or both?
- What is different about a work email to a foreign client?
- Is a formal email still important, or is it too old-fashioned?
- Would you rather get a real letter or an email? Why?
