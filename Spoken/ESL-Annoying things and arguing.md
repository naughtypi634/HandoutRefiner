# Annoying Things and Arguments

**Everyday Pet Peeves**

- What is the most annoying thing about the place where you live now?
- What annoys you most when you live at home with your family?
- Do you have a habit that you know annoys other people?
- Does it bother you when couples kiss in public?
- What are your new pet peeves from living with a phone all day?
- What annoying habit does your closest friend have?
- What kind of people annoy you the most, and why?

**Out in Public**

- Someone cuts in front of you in line — what do you do or say?
- What do people do on the subway that annoys you?
- What do people do on the street that you simply cannot stand?
- Does smoking near you bother you? What do you do about it?
- What do children do that annoys the adults around them?
- When someone is rude to a waiter, do you say anything about it?
- What is the most annoying thing you saw on your commute this week?

**Phone and Noise**

- What noise in your neighborhood drives you crazy?
- Does someone playing videos on speaker in public annoy you? What do you do?
- How do you feel when you get a work message at eleven at night?
- Are you annoyed when people take calls on a loud speaker on the bus?
- What do slow walkers who stare at their phones make you want to do?
- Does the sound of someone eating or typing loudly bother you?
- Have you ever asked a stranger to turn their music down?

**Why We Get Annoyed**

- Why do people get annoyed so easily?
- If something is annoying you, what do you usually do about it?
- Can annoying someone ever be a good thing?
- How do you tell a friend that they are being annoying?
- Who is the most annoying celebrity or public figure, and why?
- What does your own family do that pushes your buttons?
- What is an annoying catchphrase people repeat all the time?

**Arguing in Real Life**

- Do you feel bad after you have an argument with someone close to you?
- What do your family members argue about most?
- How do you avoid an argument when you feel one coming?
- Is it better to argue it out or just walk away? Why?
- Do you get along with argumentative people? Give an example.
- Are you easily persuaded in an argument, or do you hold your ground?
- How do you feel when someone disagrees with everything you say?

**Calming Down and Making Up**

- Are you passionate about your beliefs? How do you show it?
- Do you ever try to change other people's minds about politics or religion?
- Do you enjoy playing "devil's advocate" just for fun?
- Do you think it is disrespectful to argue with older people?
- After a big fight, who usually says sorry first in your family?
- What is the best way to end an argument and stay friends?
- When did you last lose your temper over something very small?
