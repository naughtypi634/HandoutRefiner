**Questions to Ask an Interviewer**

**Company Specific Questions**

- What is the management/organizational structure like here?

- Where do you see the company going in the next 5-7 years?

- Can you tell me about the company\'s future growth plans?

- What do you like best/least about working for this company?

- What is the company's culture like?

- What is one thing you would change here?

- What challenges are currently facing this company?

- What is your company\'s competitive advantage in the industry?

- Who is your biggest competitor and what advantages do they/you have?

- What is the company culture like here?

**Position Specific Questions**

- What should I expect my work schedule to be like?

- What kind of work should I expect to be doing the first year?

- What do you enjoy most about working here?

- What are some of the challenges a person in this position should
  expect to face?

- Can you describe a typical day/week/month in this position?

- Is this a new position or am I replacing someone?

- What happened to the prior person in this position?

- What does typical turnover look like in this position?

- Can I talk with the last person who held this position or one of
  his/her peers?

- Can you describe your ideal employee for this position?

- What challenges are you currently facing as a manager?

- How does this position contribute to the department and company
  overall?

**Evaluation/Training/Advancement**

- How will performance in this position be measured?

- What opportunities does the company offer for employees to maintain
  and improve their skills?

- What career paths are available for people in this position?

- Is there a formal training program for new hires?

**Benefits**

- What type of benefits (bonuses, vacation days, sick time, health
  insurance etc) do you offer?

- What is your policy regarding sick days?

- Are people allowed to work remotely?

- How much travel is expected?

**Interview Follow Up**

- Are there any gaps in my qualifications that I can improve on?

- If I am offered this position, when would you like me to start?

- Will I be hearing from you or should I contact you?

- What are the next steps in the interview process?

- What is the time line for getting back to candidates about the next
  steps for this position?

**Questions to Ask Candidates**

**Personal questions related to qualifications**

- Tell me a little about yourself.

- What are your stengths?

- What is your biggest weaknesses?

- Are you willing to travel?

- Are you willing to relocate?

- What are your short/long term goals?

- What separates you from the other candidates?

- Why should I hire you?

- How would other people describe you?

- What is one thing about yourself that you would like to change?

- Do you consider yourself a detail-oriented person or a big-picture
  person?

- Tell me about a failure and success in your life and why you think it
  happened.

- Which areas would your last boss say you need improvement?

- What accomplishment you are most proud of?

- Do you prefer working by yourself or as a part of a team?

- What do you want to improve about yourself in the next year?

**Career/job-history related questions**

- Describe your worst/best boss.

- What are your career goals?

- What did you like and dislike about your last boss?

- What were the strengths and weaknesses of your last boss?

- Tell me about your greatest professional accomplishment.

- What would you like to be doing in five years?

- What career rewards are the most important to you?

**Job and company specific questions**

- Tell me what you know about our company.

- Why did you apply for this position?

- What is your ideal working environment?

- Why do you want to work with our company?

- What makes you a good fit for this position?

- Tell me about your last job.

- Why \[did you/do you want to\] leave your \[last/current\] job?

- Do you have any questions for me?

- What type of work environment do you prefer?

- What was your biggest accomplishment in your last job?

- How much do you expect for the salary?

- What was your previous salary?

**Behavioral Questions**

- Can you give me an example of a time that you went above and beyond to
  get the job done?

- How do you keep organized?

- Tell me about a time when you disagreed with your boss and if you did
  anything about it.

- Describe a time when you couldn\'t meet a deadline and what you did.

- Tell me about a time when you were under considerable pressure to meet
  multiple assignments and what you did.

- Have you ever been on a team where someone was not doing what they
  were supposed to? How did you handle it?

- What was a major obstacle that you overcame during the past year?

- Tell me about a time where you had conflict and how you handled it.

- If you could have a do-over for your last job, what would you have
  done differently?

- Tell me a time when you had a dispute with a co-worker and how you
  handled it.

- Give me an example of a time you made a mistake and tell me how you
  handled it.

- Did you ever have an assignment that was too difficult for you, if so,
  how did you resolve the situation?

- Give me an example of an idea that you had and how you implemented it.
