# Course Introductions · 课程介绍

## Business

### 10 Hot Debate Topics For Business English Student
This week, we will debate ten hot business topics — four-day workweeks, remote work, most meetings being a waste of time, multitasking and casual dress codes. We will argue about statements like "A four-day workweek is the future" and "Employees should still come to work even if they are sick". Come and share your ideas!

### 10 Hot Debate Topics For Business English Teacher
This week, we will run ten business debates with teaching prompts. We will make lists of non-financial rewards like holidays and flexibility, rank them as a class and discuss questions like whether it's okay to exaggerate on your CV. Come and share your ideas!

### A good resume
This week, we will talk about what a good resume should look like. We will go through the dos and don'ts — keep it to one page, quantify your achievements, be honest, and skip the photo and hobbies — and decide if you agree. Come and share your ideas!

### Appropriate Language use
This week, we will talk about using appropriate language with different people. We will compare what a wife says to her husband — "Hi honey, how was your day?" — with formal lines like "Hello, how are you today?", and practice the right register for each relationship. Come and share your ideas!

### BEC-商务会议功能句型
This week, we will practice functional sentence patterns for business meetings. We will learn how to get the chairperson's attention with "May I have a word?", give opinions with "The way I see things…" and comment with "Good point!". Come and share your ideas!

### BEC-图表常用句型 incomplete
This week, we will talk about describing charts and trends. We will practice sentences like "It can be seen from the chart that…", "The general trend appears to be increases" and "…rose sharply from… to…" to compare rates and trends. Come and share your ideas!

### BEC-Questions to answer and ask in an interview
This week, we will discuss job interviews from both sides of the table. We will learn what to ask about the company — "Where do you see the company going in the next 5-7 years?" — and about the position — "What should I expect my work schedule to be like?". Come and share your ideas!

### Branding
This week, we will talk about brands and brand recall. We will play the "first brand that comes to mind" game for coffee, cars, cosmetics and travel apps, and practice saying "When I think of coffee, the first brand that comes to mind is… because…". Come and share your ideas!

### Business Discussion Questions
This week, we will discuss work and business. We will answer questions like "What helps people get a good job?" and "How will AI change work?", and share our views on choosing a company, balancing work and family, selling on WeChat and business ethics. Come and share your ideas!

### Career
This week, we will discuss careers and career paths. We will answer questions like "What is the biggest misconception about your job?" and "How did you choose your career path?", and explore personality traits from realistic to artistic. Come and share your ideas!

### Communication Skills
This week, we will talk about what communication is. We will learn the parts of the process — sender, message, channel, receiver, feedback and noise — and compare assertive, aggressive and passive styles. Come and share your ideas!

### Conflicts at work
This week, we will discuss conflicts at work. We will work through scenarios like a colleague taking credit for your idea, an unrealistic deadline and a teammate who misses deadlines, and share how you would handle each one. Come and share your ideas!

### Corporate Jargon
This week, we will talk about corporate jargon. We will go through buzzwords like "action plan", "deep dive", "buy-in" and "bandwidth", learn what they mean in English and Chinese and practice plainer alternatives. Come and share your ideas!

### Dealing with stress at work
This week, we will discuss stress at work. We will check what gives you stress — excessive workloads, tight deadlines, the glass ceiling or job insecurity — and what happens to your body, from headaches to insomnia. Come and share your ideas!

### Differences Between Chinese and Western Workplaces
This week, we will discuss six differences between Chinese and Western workplaces. We will compare company culture and banter, clearly defined roles and responsibilities, and attitudes to overtime across the two settings. Come and share your ideas!

### E-commerce Pricing strategy
This week, we will discuss e-commerce shopping and pricing. We will talk about online reviews, payment habits and return policies, then analyze strategies like value-based, penetration and dynamic pricing. Come and share your ideas!

### Email discussion topic-NaughtyPi的MacBook Pro
This week, we will talk about email habits and workplace communication. We will discuss email providers, spam, emojis in email and the very first email you sent, and why email is still so popular at work. Come and share your ideas!

### Email discussion topic
This week, we will talk about email providers and habits. We will compare providers, discuss premium services and spam, and share the story of the first email you ever sent. Come and share your ideas!

### Email Discussion
This week, we will discuss managing email overload. We will talk about folders and labels, templates for repetitive tasks, prioritizing replies and how you handle urgent emails outside work hours. Come and share your ideas!

### Email Writing Practice
This week, we will practice writing professional emails. We will write four emails — asking for clarification on a project update, requesting time off, thanking a colleague for help and following up on a task. Come and share your ideas!

### Entrepreneurship
This week, we will discuss entrepreneurship. We will look at what makes founders successful — passion, optimism, resilience and introspection — and ask who the most famous entrepreneurs in China are and what we can learn from them. Come and share your ideas!

### ESL-Body Language Basics
This week, we will talk about body language. We will read facial expressions like avoiding eye contact and raised eyebrows, and physical actions like crossed arms and nail-biting. Come and share your ideas!

### ESL-Nonviolent Communication
This week, we will discuss nonviolent communication. We will practice the four components — observation, feelings, needs and requests — and rewrite "You never do your part; you're so lazy!" into "I've noticed that some tasks are not getting done…". Come and share your ideas!

### Job Flexibility
This week, we will discuss flexible and hybrid work. We will talk about 9-5 jobs, the "3 days in office, 2 days remote" model, managing distractions at home and whether the always-on culture causes burnout. Come and share your ideas!

### Job interview questions and cheatsheet
This week, we will practice job interview questions. We will try questions like "Tell me about yourself", "Why should we hire you?" and "If you could be any kind of animal, what would you be?", then rank which ones are hardest. Come and share your ideas!

### Managing Up
This week, we will discuss managing up. We will talk about how you report to your leader — face-to-face, email or Dingding — and obstacles like lack of clarity, time issues and skill gaps. Come and share your ideas!

### Meetings – Useful or A waste of time
This week, we will debate whether meetings are useful or a waste of time. We will talk about your role in meetings, taking notes, meeting manners and whether ad-hoc meetings help or hurt. Come and share your ideas!

### Quiet quitting
This week, we will discuss quiet quitting. We will ask whether you go the extra mile or do the bare minimum, whether quiet quitting is protest or laziness, and how it affects your career growth. Come and share your ideas!

### Rookie mistakes discussion
This week, we will talk about first jobs and rookie mistakes. We will look at mistakes like asking too many questions, knowing it all and oversharing, and share the first mistake you made at work. Come and share your ideas!

### Saying No Politely
This week, we will talk about saying no politely. We will practice the three steps — a statement of regret, a reason and an alternative — with phrases like "I'd love to help you, but right now I'm really busy with…". Come and share your ideas!

### Signposts for Presentations
This week, we will talk about signposts in presentations. We will practice formal and informal phrases for introducing yourself — "On behalf of…, I'd like to welcome you" — and your topic — "I'm here today to talk about…". Come and share your ideas!

### Skills and Qualifications (A1 Level)
This week, we will talk about skills and qualifications at a beginner level. We will practice frames like "I studied…", "I learned… because…" and answer whether school and diplomas matter for getting a good job. Come and share your ideas!

### Skills and qualifications
This week, we will discuss skills and qualifications. We will look at skills that keep showing up in job ads, share your strong suits and what you need to improve, and talk about how you prefer to learn new skills. Come and share your ideas!

### Soft Transferable skills vs Hard Professional skills
This week, we will discuss soft and hard skills. We will list soft skills like teamwork, time management and adaptability, name hard skills for your job, and draw the line between the two. Come and share your ideas!

### Start-up apps
This week, we will talk about start-up apps. We will list your top five apps, complain about their biggest flaws and design an app — message, shopping, food delivery or map — from a developer's point of view. Come and share your ideas!

### Work Smarter
This week, we will discuss working smarter. We will share Calendar tricks, WeChat efficiency tips like floating windows and reminders, and try the Pomodoro Technique and Eisenhower's four quadrants. Come and share your ideas!

### Work-life balance
This week, we will discuss work-life balance. We will talk about working hours, time for hobbies, how you unwind and whether your job or your personal life is the bigger priority. Come and share your ideas!

### Working styles questionnaire_Copy
This week, we will explore working styles with a questionnaire. We will rank preferences from 1 to 4 — doing it correctly, meeting deadlines, working as a team or showing your talent — and see what your answers say about you. Come and share your ideas!

### Workplace Discrimination
This week, we will discuss workplace discrimination. We will go through vocabulary like race, pregnancy, sexual orientation, the glass ceiling and prejudice, and share our views. Come and share your ideas!

### Workplace Etiquette
This week, we will talk about workplace etiquette. We will go over the dos — respect and courtesy, active listening, avoiding gossip and communicating clearly — and practice them in conversation. Come and share your ideas!
