# Appropriate Language Use

**Tone by Relationship**

**Situation**  **Correct**  **Incorrect**
-------------  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------  -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Wife to Husband  Hi honey, how was your day? / Great, we got a lot done. And yours? / Fine, but stressful. Pass me that magazine, please.  Hello, how are you today? / I'm fine. Would you mind passing me the bread? / Certainly. Would you like some butter? / Yes, please.

Friend to Friend  Hi Charlie, can you give me a hand? / Sure, Peter. What's up? / I can't get this to work. / Why don't you try a screwdriver?  Hello Mr. Jones. May I ask you a question? / Certainly. How may I help you? / Do you think you could help me with this? / I'd be happy to help.

Subordinate to Superior  Good morning, Mr. Jones. May I ask you a question? / Certainly, how can I help you?  Good morning, Frank. I need a raise. / Do you really? Well, forget about it!

Superior to Subordinate  Excuse me, Peter. We seem to have a problem with the Smith account. / We'd better sit down and discuss it.  Hey Jack, what are you doing?! Get to work! / Hey, I'll take as much time as I need.

Coworker to Coworker  Hey, do you have a minute? I'm stuck on this report. / Sure, let me take a look. / Thanks, I owe you one.  I require your immediate assistance with this report. / I am otherwise engaged. / Kindly find someone else.

Customer to Staff  Excuse me, could you tell me where the milk is? / Of course, it's down the aisle on your right. / Thanks — is the bread down there too? / Yes, just past the eggs.  Where's the milk? / Over there.

Speaking to a Stranger  Pardon me. Do you think you could give me the time? / Certainly, it's twelve thirty. / Thank you very much. / Not at all — happy to help.  You! Tell me where the supermarket is. / There.

Candidate to Interviewer  Good morning. Thank you for seeing me — I'm here for the 10 o'clock interview.  Hi, I'm here for the interview. Where do I go?

Manager to Team  Team, please send your weekly reports by Friday 5 p.m. Thanks, everyone.  Where are the reports? I need them now!

Colleague on WeChat  Hey, are you free for five minutes? I have a quick question about the invoice.  Come to my desk. Immediately.

Customer Complaint  Excuse me, my order arrived damaged. Could you help me sort it out?  This is a joke! You people are useless!

Asking for Leave  Hi Ms Li, I'm not feeling well. May I take sick leave and work from home today?  Not coming in today lol.

Staff to Client  Good morning, Mr. Wang. Here is the updated proposal — please let me know your thoughts.  Here's the thing you wanted. Read it and get back to me.

Team Member to Manager  Hi Ms Li, I've finished the Q3 report. Could you take a look when you have time?  Report's done. Check it.

Receptionist to Guest  Good morning. May I have your name, and who are you here to see?  Who are you? Who do you want?
