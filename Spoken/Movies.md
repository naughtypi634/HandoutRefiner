# Movies

**Favorite Films**

- What are your top five favorite movies of all time?
- What was your favorite movie as a child? Do you still like it now?
- Has your taste in movies changed over the years? How?
- Do you have a favorite director or actor? Who, and why?
- What is a hidden gem you think everyone should watch? Why is it good?
- What is a movie quote you still remember by heart?
- What movie did you not enjoy at all? Why not?

**Genres**

- Name one favorite movie for each genre: action, comedy, or horror?
- Which one for romance, sci-fi, or thriller?
- Which one for documentary, animation, or musical?
- Which one for drama, fantasy, western, or kung-fu?
- Do you prefer sequels or original stories? Why?
- Cinematic universes or standalone films? Which do you like more?
- Do you care more about the story or the visual effects?

**Where You Watch**

- Where do you usually watch movies: a cinema, your phone, or a laptop?
- What is the difference between the big screen and a small one?
- When did you last go to a cinema? Who did you go with?
- Do you watch movies alone or with friends and family?
- Do you binge a whole series in one weekend, or one episode at a time?
- Do you watch foreign films with subtitles? Does that bother you?
- Have you ever fallen asleep during a movie? Which one?

**Movies and Money**

- Do you think movie stars make too much money? Why?
- Would you like to be a movie star? What kind of movies would you star in?
- Why do some movies earn billions while others fail?
- Do trailers give away too much? Do you watch them?
- Have you ever been to a film festival? What was it like?
- Would you pay extra for a premium seat or an IMAX screen?
- Should movies always have a happy ending? Why or why not?

**Movies and Us**

- What is the biggest difference between movies from different countries?
- What is the last movie you recommended to a friend? Why that one?
- Do your friends trust your taste in movies? Why or why not?
- Which movie made you laugh, cry, or think deeply? What happened?
- If your life were a movie, what genre would it be?
- Would you rather rewatch a favorite or try something new?
- Is it better to know nothing about a movie before you watch it?

**Cinema Today**

- Do you watch movies legally or find them online? Is that a problem?
- Short videos or full movies — which do you watch more now?
- Are you on your phone during a movie? Does it ruin it?
- What kind of movie would you like to see made about your city?
- Do you ever watch the same movie twice? Why?
