- What was your first job?

- How did you prepare for that job? What worried you the most back then?
  Was it money, commute, rent, interest, or something else?

- Did your college major relate to your first job? What about your
  current job?

- Do you feel safe in your workplace? Is it OK to make mistakes? How do
  you feel when you make a mistake?

- What are some common rookie mistakes in your field/position?

  - Asking Too Many Questions

  - Knowing It All

  - Over-Explaining Everything You Do

  - Answering "Yes" or "No" Before Thinking

  - Oversharing Personal Information

  - be too eager to impress

- What kinds of mistakes are acceptable and which ones are not in
  different jobs?

- What was the very first mistake you made in your career? How did you
  fix it? Anyone helped you? Did you report it right away or did you try
  to fix it on your own first?

- Have you seen a newbie make the same mistake you did? How did you
  feel? What did you do?

- If you're a senior mentor, what can you do to make sure the rookies
  have a good transition?
