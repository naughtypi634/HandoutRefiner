# Travel Hacks

**Travel Vocabulary**

**Scenery and Experiences**

- impressive (adjective): very good and worth noticing.
- stunning (adjective): extremely beautiful or impressive.
- breathtaking (adjective): so beautiful that it surprises you.
- memorable (adjective): special and easy to remember.
- scenic (adjective): having beautiful natural views.
- peaceful (adjective): quiet and calm.

**Places and Culture**

- exotic (adjective): unusual and interesting because it comes from another place.
- unique (adjective): different from anything else.
- authentic (adjective): real and traditional, not made for tourists.
- historic (adjective): important because of its history.
- cultural (adjective): connected with the life, art, and traditions of a place.
- local (adjective): connected with the people and businesses of a particular area.

**Travel Conditions**

- convenient (adjective): easy and useful for a particular situation.
- crowded (adjective): full of people.
- remote (adjective): far from cities and other busy places.
- touristy (adjective): popular with tourists and designed for tourists.
- flexible (adjective): easy to change when plans change.
- affordable (adjective): not too expensive.
