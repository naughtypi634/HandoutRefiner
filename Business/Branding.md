**Brand Words 品牌词汇**

**Word 词**             **中文**                          **Example 例句**
----------------------  --------------------------------  ---------------------------------
knockoff                山寨货 · 仿冒品                   "It's a knockoff — half the price."
dupe                    平替 · 平价替代                   "This bag is a dupe of a designer one."
brand loyalty           品牌忠诚度                        "I have zero brand loyalty. I compare prices."
flagship store          旗舰店                            "They just opened one downtown."
spokesperson            代言人                            "She's the new face of the brand."
slogan                  广告语 · 广告口号                  "What's the slogan? I can't remember it."
packaging               包装                              "Nice packaging, same product."
warranty                保修                              "Two years' warranty, no questions asked."
refund                  退款                              "I asked for a refund the next day."
coupon                  优惠券                            "It's only cheap with a coupon."
reviews                 用户评价                          "I read the reviews before I buy."
free returns            免费退换                          "Free returns, so I'll just try it."

**Discussion 讨论**

- What factors do you consider when you choose a brand?
- Why are some brands much more expensive than others?
- Have you ever switched brands after a bad experience?
- Would you pay more for a Chinese brand, or a foreign one?
- Do you buy the same brands as your parents? Why or why not?
- Which brand has the best ads in China right now?
- Have you ever bought a knockoff or a dupe 平替? Was it worth it?
- Which Chinese brand do you think could go global next?
