**Personal Travel Experience**

Experience

- Have you ever been abroad? How many times? Which countries/ cities?

- When and where was the last time you traveled?

- What was your best trip?

- What was your worst trip?

Difficult Travel Situations

- Have you ever been in a difficult situation while traveling? What was
  it?

**Travel Preferences and Styles**

Travel Companions

- Do you prefer to travel alone or in a group? Why?

Transportation

- Do you prefer to travel by train, bus, plane or ship? Why?

- Do you travel with a lot of baggage, or do you like to travel light?

Travel Season

- Which season is the best for travel?

Holiday Type

- Do you prefer active or relaxing holidays? Why?

Accommodation

- Would you prefer to stay at a hotel or camp while on vacation?

Budget

- Where do you spend your budget?

**Destinations and Planning**

Domestic Destinations

- What is the most interesting city to visit in China?

- Do you have any hot takes?

International Destinations

- What countries would you like to visit?

Future Plans

- Are you planning on going anywhere for your next vacation?

  - If so, where?

  - Who with?

  - How long will you stay?

- Will you ever go back to the same place?

**Travel Motivation and Meaning**

Why Travel

- Why do people travel? What are some good things about travel?
