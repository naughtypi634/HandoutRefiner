**[MBTI]{.underline}**

Answer the following questions with "Yes" or "No" to determine your
personality type.

Do you find it easy to introduce yourself to new people? (Yes = E, No =
I)

Do you focus more on facts and details than ideas and possibilities?
(Yes = S, No = N)

Do you make decisions based on logic rather than emotions? (Yes = T, No
= F)

Do you prefer a structured plan over spontaneous decision? (Yes = J, No
= P)
