# Career

**Personality and Fit**

- Which fits you best: realistic, investigative, artistic, or social?
- Would you rather lead a team or solve problems on your own?
- Do you like hands-on work with machines, or work with ideas and people?
- Are you detail-oriented, or do you care more about the big picture?
- What kind of work makes you lose track of time?
- Do you prefer a fixed routine, or a job where every day is different?
- Is your job a good fit for your personality? Why?

**Your Job**

- What do you do for work? What are your main responsibilities?
- What does a normal workday look like, from first email to last?
- What do you enjoy most about your job?
- What is the hardest part of your job, and how do you handle it?
- What do people usually get wrong about what you do?
- What is one small thing at work that makes your day better?
- How would you describe your job to a friend in one minute?

**Your Path**

- What job did you want when you first started working? Did you get it?
- How did you end up in the career you are in now?
- Was your first job a good start, or a mistake? Why?
- Is your job what you expected? What surprised you most?
- Have you ever changed jobs? What made you decide to leave?
- Who helped you most when you were choosing your path?

**Skills and Growth**

- Which skills matter most in your line of work?
- What is one skill you had to learn the hard way?
- Are you learning anything new for work right now?
- Is it harder to learn new things as you get older? Why?
- Would you take a course if your company paid for it?
- What would you study if you had a free year?
- Do you learn more from a mentor, a course, or by doing?

**Money and Success**

- Is a high salary the most important thing in a job? Why?
- Would you take a lower-paid job you love over one you hate?
- How do you decide when it is time to ask for a raise?
- What does "success" mean to you in your career?
- Have you ever been promoted? How did it change your work?
- Is it better to be a specialist or a generalist today?
- What would make you quit a job tomorrow?

**Looking Ahead**

- What are your long-term career goals?
- Where do you see yourself in five years?
- How do you think your job will change in ten years?
- Could AI take over part of your job? Which part?
- What advice would you give someone starting in your field?
- If you could start over, would you choose a different career?
