# 100 Idioms

## 工作职场

all in the same boat
处境相同
Everyone on my team has to work late this week, so we're all in the same boat.

burn the midnight oil
熬夜赶工
I burned the midnight oil on the slides, so I can't keep my eyes open in the meeting.

call it a day
收工
We finished the slides at 9 p.m., so we called it a day and went home.

cut corners
偷工减料
The old building cut corners on the walls, so water now leaks into my room.

drop the ball
搞砸了
I dropped the ball and forgot to renew my metro card, so I got stuck at the gate.

get the ball rolling
开个头
We've planned enough; let's get the ball rolling and write the first page today.

go the extra mile
多走一步，加倍用心
The delivery rider went the extra mile and carried my heavy box up to the sixth floor.

in hot water
惹上麻烦
He's in hot water because he sent the report three days after the deadline.

nip something in the bud
及早制止
My manager nipped the rumor in the bud by posting the real numbers in the group chat.

on thin ice
处境危险
He's on thin ice at work; one more mistake and he could lose the job.

show someone the ropes
带新手入门
On my first day, my colleague showed me the ropes; how to book a room and where to get tea.

the ball is in your court
轮到你决定
I sent my resume to the HR manager; now the ball is in her court.

up in the air
悬而未决
The weekend trip is still up in the air; we're waiting for the weather report.

## 人际沟通

all ears
洗耳恭听
I finished my work early, so I'm all ears; tell me about your interview.

barking up the wrong tree
找错对象，白费力气
If you think I took your phone, you're barking up the wrong tree; I stayed home all evening.

beat around the bush
拐弯抹角
Stop beating around the bush and tell me why you missed the meeting.

bury the hatchet
和好，冰释前嫌
After arguing for a week, my roommate and I buried the hatchet over hotpot.

get a taste of your own medicine
自食其果
He always hides my mouse, so he got a taste of his own medicine when I hid his keyboard.

get your wires crossed
搞误会
We got our wires crossed; I thought dinner was Friday, but it was Saturday.

give someone the benefit of the doubt
姑且相信
My coworker said the printer ate her report, and I gave her the benefit of the doubt this time.

hit the nail on the head
一针见血
My mom hit the nail on the head; I stayed up playing games instead of studying.

let the cat out of the bag
说漏嘴
My brother let the cat out of the bag about the surprise cake, and now Mom knows.

make a long story short
长话短说
To make a long story short, I missed the high-speed rail and had to buy another ticket.

on the same page
意见一致
My roommate and I finally got on the same page about the cleaning schedule, so we stopped arguing.

pull someone's leg
开玩笑逗人
My coworker said the elevator is broken, but he was just pulling my leg; it works fine.

put your foot in your mouth
说错话
I put my foot in my mouth when I asked my coworker about the baby; she isn't pregnant.

take a rain check
改天再说
I can't make dinner tonight because of overtime; can I take a rain check?

## 情绪感受

at the end of your rope
忍无可忍
After waiting thirty minutes for a delivery that never came, I was at the end of my rope.

cold feet
临阵退缩
I planned to quit my job, but I got cold feet before sending the email.

drive someone up the wall
把人逼疯
My neighbor's dog barking at midnight drives me up the wall.

get a grip
冷静点
Get a grip; it's just one bad score, not the end of the world.

get bent out of shape
火大，气得不行
My roommate gets bent out of shape when I leave my shoes by the door.

get off on the wrong foot
一开始就不顺
We got off on the wrong foot; I spilled coffee on my new colleague on her first day.

in the heat of the moment
一时冲动
I said something rude to my boss in the heat of the moment, and now I regret it.

keep your chin up
别灰心
You failed the test twice, but keep your chin up; the third time is often lucky.

like a fish out of water
格格不入
I felt like a fish out of water at the formal dinner with my girlfriend's colleagues.

on cloud nine
开心极了
When my boss approved my Spring Festival leave, I was on cloud nine.

put someone on the spot
让人下不来台
My boss put me on the spot and asked for the answer right there in the meeting.

throw someone for a loop
让人措手不及
The interviewer asked about my salary plans, and it threw me for a loop.

## 态度观点

at the drop of a hat
二话不说
My mom comes to help at the drop of a hat; I just send one WeChat message.

cross that bridge when you come to it
到时再说
I don't know what to do if the landlord raises the rent, but I'll cross that bridge when I come to it.

cry over spilled milk
后悔也没用
My phone is broken, and crying over spilled milk won't fix it; I just ordered a new one.

go with the flow
顺其自然
I don't plan my weekends; I just go with the flow and see who is free.

jump the gun
操之过急
He jumped the gun and told everyone the news before the boss approved it.

no-brainer
想都不用想
When the app offered free same-day delivery, choosing it was a no-brainer.

not my cup of tea
不对胃口
Horror movies are not my cup of tea; I watch them with my eyes half closed.

on the fence
犹豫不决
I'm on the fence about the new phone; mine still works, but the camera is old.

play it by ear
随机应变
We haven't booked anything for the trip; we'll play it by ear when we arrive.

put the cart before the horse
本末倒置
I booked the hotel before asking for leave; I put the cart before the horse.

save your breath
别白费口舌
Save your breath; my mom has already decided where we'll go for the holiday.

shoot yourself in the foot
搬起石头砸自己的脚
I told my boss I was free, and he gave me the biggest project; I shot myself in the foot.

silver lining
不幸中的万幸
I failed the driving test, but the silver lining is that my dad and I get more practice rides together.

take it with a grain of salt
半信半疑
He said the salary is very high, but take it with a grain of salt; it's just a rumor.

turn a blind eye
睁一只眼闭一只眼
The teacher turned a blind eye when students checked their phones in class.

## 生活日常

a night owl
夜猫子
I'm a night owl; I do my best work after 11 p.m.

bring home the bacon
养家糊口
She does live-streaming at night to bring home the bacon for her family.

cost an arm and a leg
贵得离谱
Renting an apartment near the metro costs an arm and a leg, so I moved two stops away.

eyeball it
目测
I had no ruler, so I eyeballed the shelf width before buying it online.

jump on the bandwagon
跟风
Everyone at my office uses the new AI tool, so I jumped on the bandwagon too.

kick the bucket
去世（口语）
He jokes that he wants to eat hotpot in Chongqing before he kicks the bucket.

kill two birds with one stone
一举两得
I listen to English podcasts on the metro, so I kill two birds with one stone on the way to work.

once in a blue moon
极少发生
I cook at home most days and order takeout once in a blue moon, only when I work late.

out of the blue
突然，毫无预兆
My college classmate called me out of the blue yesterday after five years.

pipe down
安静点
Pipe down, everyone; the teacher is about to start.

spice things up
换换花样
We always eat at home, so let's spice things up and try the new hotpot place.

the best of both worlds
两全其美
Working from home gives me the best of both worlds; no crowded commute and more time with my cat.

under the weather
身体不舒服
I felt under the weather this morning, so I called my boss and worked from home.

## 压力挑战

between a rock and a hard place
进退两难
I'm between a rock and a hard place; the job pays well, but the office is far away.

bite the bullet
硬着头皮做
My air conditioner broke in July, so I bit the bullet and called the landlord at 11 p.m.

elephant in the room
避而不谈的大问题
Everyone knows the project is late, but nobody mentions it; it's the elephant in the room.

face the music
承担后果
I arrived late again, so I have to face the music and explain to my boss.

go down in flames
彻底失败
The live-streaming sale went down in flames; only three people watched.

hang in there
坚持住
The gym is hard at first, but hang in there; you'll see changes after a month.

off the hook
脱身，不用担责
The meeting was canceled, so I'm off the hook and can go home early.

the last straw
最后一根稻草
The slow elevator was the last straw; I finally moved out of that building.

throw caution to the wind
豁出去，不顾后果
She threw caution to the wind and spent a month's salary on a trip to Yunnan.

throw in the towel
认输放弃
After three failed interviews, she threw in the towel and looked for a job in another city.

when it rains, it pours
祸不单行
My bike chain broke in the morning, and then my phone died; when it rains, it pours.

## 学习成长

by the skin of your teeth
差一点，侥幸
I caught the last train by the skin of my teeth; the doors closed right behind me.

it's a piece of cake
小菜一碟
Ordering food on WeChat is a piece of cake; even my six-year-old niece can do it.

lose your touch
手生了
I haven't cooked for months, so I've lost my touch; the noodles were too salty.

not ring a bell
没印象
He says we met at a party, but his face doesn't ring a bell.

play devil's advocate
唱反调
Everyone likes the plan, but let me play devil's advocate; what if the app crashes on launch day?

rule of thumb
经验法则
My rule of thumb is to order takeout before 11:30, or I wait forty minutes for lunch.

swallow something hook, line, and sinker
全盘相信
My son said the dog ate his homework, and I almost swallowed it hook, line, and sinker.

wrap your head around something
想明白，弄懂
It took me a week to wrap my head around the new scheduling app.
