**Travel Hacks**

**[Before traveling]{.underline}**

1.  What do we absolutely need to check? Weather, Passport expiration,
    luggage weight...

2.  Do you travel light or do you overpack?

3.  What is on your packing list? What is a must and what is a
    definitely no for you?

**[Air travel]{.underline}**

1.  How can we be on time for our flights?

2.  What do you do during the flight? What things do you need? Foot
    pillows, travel pillows, sleeping/eye masks.

**[Public transport]{.underline}**

1.  How do we get familiar with the local public transport?

2.  Anything we should be careful about?

**[Sightseeing]{.underline}**

1.  Roaming or itinerary-based trip?

2.  Do you leave some space for unexpected changes, or do you fill up
    all your time slots?

3.  Your idea on "Visit Everywhere All At Once" and "Snap a XXX-worthy
    photo and go at [scenic spots/tourist attractions"]{.underline}

4.  Do you know any tourist traps? How do we avoid them?

5.  Where can we go besides the beaten paths? What do you think of the
    guided (group) tours?

**[Food/Restaurant]{.underline}**

1.  How can we find better local restaurants?

2.  Anything we should avoid?

**[Hotel]{.underline}**

1.  There are many free services that we can use from the hotel, just
    ask away.

2.  Your worst hotel experience and how you think you can avoid it next
    time.
