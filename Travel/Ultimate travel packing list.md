**Carry-on versus checked luggage**

Generally speaking, there are a few items that will never be allowed in
your carry-on bags, including:

- Sharp items (e.g. scissors, razor blades and knives)

- Flammable materials (e.g. lighters, matches and aerosols)

- Potential weapons (e.g. cricket bats, golf clubs and hammers)

- Liquids exceeding a certain volume

- Some food and drink.

 

**Clothes to pack**

**Think about your destination's climate and cultural norms**\
For example, four pairs of jeans for a two-week Hawaiian getaway will
add up to unnecessary weight, and mini-skirts may not be suitable for
conservative countries.

**Discussion**

Habits & Philosophy

- Do you pack light or bring everything just in case?

- What\'s your strategy for deciding what to pack for different weather?

- Do you use a packing list, or is it all in your head?

- How many of the clothes you pack do you actually end up wearing? 

Essential Items & \"Must-Haves\"

- What are the things you absolutely cannot travel without?

- What\'s the most unusual or unexpected item you\'ve ever packed?

- Do you pack for entertainment (books, games) or rely on your
  phone/tablet?

Travel Mishaps & Solutions

- Have you ever forgotten something crucial, like your passport or
  medication? What happened?

- What\'s the biggest packing mistake you\'ve ever made? 

Packing for Specific Trips

- If you could only bring five things for a month-long trip, what would
  they be?

- How do you pack differently for a beach vacation versus a city break?

- For a long flight, what do you pack in your carry-on for comfort?
