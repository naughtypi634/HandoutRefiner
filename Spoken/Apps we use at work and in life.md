# Apps We Use at Work and in Life

**Your Phone**

- How many apps do you have on your phone right now?
- How many of them did you actually open this week?
- What's your screen time this week? Was it higher than you thought?
- Which app do you open first in the morning?
- Which app eats up most of your time?
- Which app did you install and then never open again?
- Do you ever turn your phone off on purpose? What happens?

**Social and Messaging**

- Which app do you text your friends on?
- How many group chats are you in? Which one never stops?
- Do you actually call people, or do you just send a message?
- Which app do you post on, if you post at all?
- How much short video do you watch in a day?
- Do you mute your group chats at night? Which ones?
- Do you post on Moments, or just read other people's posts?

**Apps at Work**

- Which apps does your job make you use?
- Which one would you never put on your own phone?
- Do you get work messages after hours? On which app?
- Which app makes your job easier?
- Which one is the most annoying?
- Do you answer your work group at 11 p.m.? What happens if you don't?
- Do you use a company phone or your own for work?

**Apps for Food, Money and Getting Around**

- Which app do you use to order food?
- How do you pay when you go out: Alipay, WeChat Pay, or cash?
- Which app do you use to get around town?
- Do you shop on Taobao, Pinduoduo, or Xiaohongshu? Which is cheapest?
- Do you track your spending with an app? Does it change anything?
- Do you book train tickets in an app or on a website?
- Have you ever split a bill in an app after dinner? How did you do it?

**Apps for Health and Study**

- Do you have a fitness or health app? Does it actually change anything?
- Do you track your sleep? Do you trust the numbers?
- Is there an app you use to study or learn English?
- Do you keep a to-do list on your phone? What's on it today?
- Have you ever tried an app to cut down your screen time?
- Do you listen to music or podcasts in an app? When?
- Which app helps you most with your daily routine?

**Apps You'd Recommend**

- Name one app you think everyone should use. Why?
- Which popular app do you think is overrated?
- Which app did you try and then delete?
- If you had to delete one app forever, which one goes?
- Do you ever wonder what your apps know about you?
- How much do you pay for subscriptions in a month?
- If you could design your own app, what would it do?
