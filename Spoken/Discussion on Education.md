# Discussion on Education

**Personal Learning Habits**

- Are you studying or learning anything these days? What is it, and how many hours a week do you spend on it compared with your school days?
- Do you enjoy learning new things? Give a recent example.
- What is your learning style? Do you learn best by listening, reading, doing things, or something else?
- Do you go to the library? What kind of study environment do you prefer?
- Do you listen to music while you study? What kind of music helps you focus?
- Do you prefer group study or studying alone? Which one suits you better?
- For how many hours can you study before you get tired or lose focus?
- Does studying ever exhaust you? Can it be as tiring as physical labor, and how do you get your energy back?
- Have you earned any certificates or awards through studying that you are proud of?
- What gives you the motivation to study new things?

**School Days and Reflection**

- Do you remember a typical day as a high school student? What was it like, and do you feel nostalgic about it?
- What were your favorite and least favorite subjects in school? Which ones did you find easy, and which were difficult?
- Did you learn any life skills at school, such as cooking or managing money?
- What do you think schools should teach but currently do not?
- Is there anything you studied at school that you now think was a waste of time?
- If you could go back in time, what would you do differently in your school life?

**Ways of Learning and Teachers**

- There are many ways to learn these days: courses, private tutors, online videos. Which way do you prefer, and what are the pros and cons?
- How do you feel about studying online? Is it better than learning in a classroom?
- What have you learned on your own, without a teacher or formal education?
- Would you like to study overseas? Where would you go, and what would you study?
- Who has taught you a lot in your life?
- How important is good parenting when it comes to education? Do you have any personal experience to share?
- Have you ever considered becoming a teacher? Why or why not?
- Is a teacher necessary for learning? Why or why not?

**Exams and the Education System**

- How do you feel about tests and exams? Are they necessary?
- Are standardized tests a fair way to measure a student's knowledge? Why or why not?
- Do you think scoring well on standardized tests leads to a successful career?
- Recently, many high schools in China announced a "two days off" policy. What do you think of it?
- Do we have a good education system in China? What are its strengths and weaknesses, and which schools are considered the best?

**Education, Society and the Future**

- What does education mean to you?
- How important is education for an individual, a society and a country?
- What are the three most important subjects to study, and why?
- How many years do you have to study something before you become an expert at it?
- What are the elderly and the young learning now? What can they learn from each other?
- What kinds of things will be important to learn in the next ten years?
- How can technology, such as AI, improve the way students learn in the future?
- Do you prefer to spend time with people of a similar level of education to yourself?
