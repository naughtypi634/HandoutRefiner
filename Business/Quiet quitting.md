# Quiet Quitting

**Employee Perspective**

- What are your working hours? When do you actually start working?
- How much of the workday do you spend actually working?
- Have you ever gone the extra mile, or do you do the bare minimum?
- Do you answer work messages after hours, or leave them for tomorrow?
- How do you feel about overtime — do you take it or avoid it?
- Do you volunteer for new projects, or only do what the job requires?
- Do you feel loyal to your company, or are you just clocking in?
- Do you join team events and mentoring, or quietly skip them?

**In the Office**

- What do you do during your lunch break? Is it really a break?
- How engaged are you in meetings — do you speak or just listen?
- Do you update your boss on progress, or wait for the weekly meeting?
- Do you reply to every message, or only the important ones?
- When was the last time you learned a new skill for work?
- Do you leave exactly on time, or stay late without being asked?
- Do you take your full holiday days, or save them for later?

**Why People Do It**

- Is quiet quitting a protest, a way to set limits, or just laziness?
- Are people quiet quitting, or just burned out? How can you tell?
- Does a bad manager push people into quiet quitting?
- Is a fair salary enough to keep people working hard?
- Does quiet quitting hurt the team, or only the company?
- Would you tell your boss if a colleague quietly quit?

**Career and Money**

- Can good pay stop quiet quitting? What actually motivates you?
- Does quiet quitting hurt your career growth in the long run?
- Is it possible to work quietly and still get promoted?
- Would you rather be the hardest worker or the happiest one?
- Have you ever quietly quit a job before you actually left it?
- Does changing jobs solve the problem, or make it worse?
- What would your ideal workday include to keep you engaged?

**Employer Perspective**

- How can a company spot quiet quitting before people leave?
- Should managers punish quiet quitters or try to win them back?
- Is quiet quitting contagious? Can it spread through a team?
- How does the culture change when people stop going the extra mile?
- How can a manager rebuild a team's energy after a hard year?
- Would more flexible hours keep people engaged?
- Should a company fix work-life balance before anything else?
- Do clear career paths stop people from quietly quitting?

**What Would Fix It**

- Do regular one-on-one check-ins actually help? Why?
- Would better pay and recognition make any real difference?
- How should a manager talk to someone who looks burned out?
- Does remote work make quiet quitting easier or harder?
- What is one rule a company could change to fix this?
- Would you trust a company that suddenly promised to change?
