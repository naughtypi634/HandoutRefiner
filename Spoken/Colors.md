**Challenge**

Find all the colors within sight

**Conversation questions**

- What is your favorite color? Has it changed over the years? Why?

- What colors do you think about when you buy clothes? What about shoes?

- What are your thoughts on colors for interior and furniture?

- If you want to buy a car, what color would you choose?

- What color do you think best represents your personality, and why?

- What colors do you dislike? Why?

- Are you good at matching colors? Which colors go well together?

- Do colors affect your mood? Give an example if you can.

- Have you ever dyed your hair a different color? Why did you change it?
  What color did you go?

- Have you ever met someone who was color blind? What colors couldn't
  they see?

- What is the most popular and important color in your city? What color
  are most houses?

- Which cultures in the world do you think are very colorful? Why do you
  think so?

- What things cause people to *go red in the face/ see red*? What about
  you?

- What makes you feel *blue*?

- Do you do anything to make your skin lighter or darker?

- Some people think pink is a feminine color. Is it okay for men to wear
  pink?

- Does color have anything to do with your favorite brand?
