# Travel Experience Speaking Practice

**Preparation**

- Where did you go, and which cities or places did you visit?
- When did you go, and how long did you stay?
- Who did you travel with: alone, family, or friends?
- What did you do? Pick three or four main activities.
- How did you feel during the trip?

**Opening**

- I want to tell you about my trip to [place].
- Last [time], I visited [place] with [who].
- I'd like to share my travel experience with you.

**Main Story**

- First, I ...
- Then, I ...
- After that, ...
- One day, ...
- The best part was when ...
- I remember ...

**Details**

- It was [hot / cold / crowded / quiet].
- I saw, heard, and tasted ...
- The place looked like ...
- It smelled like ...
- I was surprised because ...
- I couldn't believe ...
- I felt amazing when ...

**Closing**

- It was an unforgettable experience.
- I learned so much about the culture.
- I would definitely go back.
- Overall, I really enjoyed it because ...

**Vocabulary**

- Adjectives — beautiful, crowded, friendly, historical, peaceful.
- Past-tense verbs — visited, explored, discovered, tried, climbed.
- Time expressions — last summer, on the first day, in the morning.
- Emotions — excited, surprised, nervous, amazed, grateful.
