# Talking About Travel

**Travel Plans**

- Have you ever been abroad? How many times, and which countries?
- Are you planning on going anywhere for your next vacation?
- If so, where, with whom, and how long will you stay?
- Are you afraid of going abroad alone? Why or why not?
- Do you prefer to travel alone or in a group? Why?
- Do you like to travel with children, parents, friends, or alone?
- What do you need before you can travel to another country?

**Getting There**

- Do you prefer to travel by train, bus, plane, or ship? Why?
- Do you prefer summer vacations or winter vacations? Why?
- Do you travel with a lot of baggage, or do you travel light?
- What luggage do you usually carry, and what do you always take?
- Have you ever taken a package tour, or do you book it yourself?
- Would you prefer to stay at a hotel, a homestay, or camp?
- Do you prefer a budget hotel or a first-class hotel? Why?

**Past Trips**

- What was your best trip?
- What was your worst trip?
- What's the most beautiful place you've ever been to?
- Where did you spend your last vacation?
- When did you last travel, and where did you go?
- Have you ever gotten lost while traveling? Tell us about it.
- Have you ever been in a difficult situation while traveling?

**Places and Paperwork**

- If you traveled to Europe or South America, where would you go?
- What are some countries that you would never visit? Why not?
- What is the most interesting city to visit in your country?
- What are popular tourist destinations in your country, and have you been?
- Which one would you recommend to a first-time visitor, and why?
- What is the most interesting souvenir you have ever bought on a trip?
- How do you learn about a new place before you book a trip?

**Holiday Style**

- Do you prefer active holidays or relaxing holidays? Why?
- Would you rather be somewhere crowded, or somewhere quiet?
- Would you like to go back to the same place, or try somewhere new?
- What do you do on holiday when the weather is bad?
- What is the best holiday for children, adults, and the elderly?
- Do you prefer to sightsee all day, or take it slow?

**Why We Travel**

- When you were a child, did your family take a vacation every year?
- Who makes the decisions when your family plans a holiday?
- Is there any difference between young tourists and adult tourists?
- Why do you travel? Why do people travel, in your opinion?
- What are some benefits of travel?
- What is one trip you would love to take in the next five years?
