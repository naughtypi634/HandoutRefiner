# Home

**Your Home Now**

- Do you live in a house or an apartment? How many rooms are there?
- Do you have your own room, or do you share one with family?
- What is your favorite corner at home, and why do you sit there?
- Is your home close to your work or school? How long is the commute?
- Do you rent or own your home? Who decided to live there?
- What would you change about your home if you could?
- Who does the cleaning and cooking at your place?

**Big or Small**

- Would you rather live in a big house or a small one? Why?
- Is it better for each person to have their own room?
- How many bathrooms does a home need? Is one ever enough?
- Does a small home feel cozy or crowded to you?
- What is one thing a big house has that a small one cannot give?
- Would you give up space to live in a better location?
- Is a big house worth all the cleaning and the cost?

**Rent or Buy**

- Is it better to rent a home or buy one? What do you think?
- Housing in your city keeps getting pricier. Is buying still worth it?
- What are the hidden costs of renting that people forget?
- Would you take a long mortgage to own your own place?
- Have you ever moved house? How did the move go?
- What would you tell a friend who is about to sign a lease?
- Would you buy a place near work, or far away and cheaper?

**Where to Live**

- Do you prefer living in the city or a village? Why?
- Is life easier in the city, or quieter in the countryside?
- Would you live close to your parents, or far away for work?
- Should grown children live near their family? Why or why not?
- How often do you visit your family? Is it enough?
- Would you move to another city for a better job?
- What makes a neighborhood feel like home to you?

**Pets, Gadgets and Safety**

- Do you keep a pet at home? A cat, a dog, or something else?
- Are pets good for a family? What do they add to a home?
- Would you live in a smart home you control from your phone?
- Do you have a space to work or study at home? Is it quiet?
- Do you live in a gated community that controls who comes in?
- Are security cameras around the building a good thing or not?
- Would you rather have a smaller home in a safer block?

**Home and Family Life**

- Is your home often full of people, or usually quiet?
- Do you like having guests stay overnight?
- How do you and your family split the housework?
- Do your parents visit you, or do you always go to them?
- What is your favorite family memory that happened at home?
- Does the word "home" mean a place to you, or the people?
