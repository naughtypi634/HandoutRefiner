# Healthy Lifestyle

**Eat Well**

- Fill half your plate with vegetables, and add fruit every day.
- Drink about 8 cups of water — keep a bottle on your desk at work.
- Pick whole grains like oats or brown rice over white rice.
- Cut back on milk tea, cola, and late-night fried snacks.
- Eat one home-cooked meal a day instead of takeout.

**Move More**

- Aim for 150 minutes of exercise a week — about 30 minutes, five days.
- Walk or cycle for short trips instead of taking a car or the subway.
- Take the stairs when you are only going up a few floors.
- Do two strength sessions a week: push-ups, squats, or gym time.
- Stand up and stretch every hour if you sit at a desk all day.

**Sleep Well**

- Try to sleep 7 to 9 hours a night.
- Go to bed and get up at the same time, weekends included.
- Put your phone face down an hour before bed.
- Keep the room dark, quiet, and a little cool.
- Skip coffee after 3 p.m. if you toss and turn at night.

**Mind and Mood**

- Take ten quiet minutes a day — breathe, or just sit with tea.
- Write down three good things from your day before bed.
- Talk to someone when you feel stressed instead of holding it in.
- Take a real lunch break instead of eating at your desk.
- Say no to extra shifts when you are already running on empty.

**People Around You**

- Call your parents or a close friend at least once a week.
- Join one group — a run club, a dance class, a board-game night.
- Find a health buddy so you both keep showing up.
- Make time for coffee with a friend, not just WeChat messages.
- Ask for help early when you are struggling.

**Stay on Top of It**

- Go for a check-up once a year, even when you feel fine.
- Keep your vaccinations up to date.
- Track your sleep, steps, or mood in your phone for a month.
- Keep chips and cola out of the cupboard at home.
- Check in with yourself each month and fix one small thing.

**Discussion**

- Do you think you have a healthy lifestyle? Why or why not?
- What did you eat yesterday? Was any of it bad for you?
- How much do you sleep on a normal night? Is that enough?
- How do you fit exercise into a busy work week?
- What is your worst health habit, and why is it hard to stop?
- What do you do when you feel stressed or burned out?
- Do you drink enough water, or mostly tea and coffee?
- How often do you order takeout? Do you want to change that?
- Who keeps you motivated to stay healthy? How do they do it?
- Is it easy to eat well in your city, or is takeout everywhere?
- Do you go for a yearly check-up, or only when you feel sick?
- How much time do you spend sitting each day? Does it worry you?
- What do you do to relax after a long day?
- Do you exercise alone or with friends? Which works better for you?
- What would you change first to live a healthier life?
- Do your parents worry about your health? What do they say?
- Have you ever tried a diet or a fitness app? How did it go?
- What is one small healthy habit you could start this week?
