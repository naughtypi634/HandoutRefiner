**Dos**

- Pack light/essentials: Necessities, toiletries, meds; carry-on extras,
  roll clothes.

- A comfortable pair of shoes

- Stay hydrated/eat well: Water, snacks; regular meals.

- Wash hands often: Use sanitizer frequently.

- Learn key phrases: Greetings, questions, thanks.

- Dress properly: Follow norms; polite interactions.

- Keep documents ready: Print tickets, hotel; physical copies.

- Split cash/items: Hidden pouches; trackers in all bags.

- Check weather/news/laws: Customs, tipping, warnings.

- Get vaccines/meds: Consult doctor; adjust time zone (sleep on plane,
  no long naps).

- Be careful about public Wi-Fi: Protect data; local SIM.

- Research culture/destination: Avoid taboos (禁忌); unique laws (e.g.,
  driving side).

- Prioritize safety: Aware surroundings; reliable transport; emergency
  contacts.

- Register with embassy.

- Respect wildlife: No feeding/touching; be respectful/polite.

- An open attitude - to truly experience and appreciate a new culture,
  it requires an open mind.

- Your suggestions.

**Don\'ts**

- Overpack: Only needs; avoid burden/loss.

- Show valuables: Keep low-key; eye on belongings.

- Drink tap water: If unsafe.

- Overdrink alcohol: Know limits; no risks.

- Leave bags unattended: Especially crowded areas.

- Overschedule: Allow flexibility. Don't micromanage your itinerary:
  Don\'t try to do and see everything, and be open to spontaneous
  opportunities

- Discuss politics/religion: If sensitive.

- Exchange money unofficially.

- Disclose too much information: Avoid posting photos of important
  documents like passports or boarding passes and be careful about
  sharing your precise itinerary publicly.

- Rely on one technology: Don\'t rely solely on credit cards or Wi-Fi,
  as you may need cash or an international SIM card in many places.

- Take photos without permission/ consent.
