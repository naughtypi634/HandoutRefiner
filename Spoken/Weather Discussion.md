# Weather

**Favorite Weather**

- What's your favorite type of weather? Why do you like it?
- What's your favorite thing to do on a sunny day?
- Do you like warm or cool weather for outdoor activities?
- What's your worst kind of weather — too hot, too cold, or too wet?
- Which city has weather that suits you best?
- Would you rather have a sunny beach day or a snowy day?
- What's the perfect weather for a walk in the park?

**Weather and Mood**

- How does the weather affect your mood? Give an example from this week.
- Does sunshine make you feel happier? Why do you think so?
- How do you feel on a gray, cloudy day?
- Does rain ever make you feel calm or sleepy?
- Are you in a good mood when it snows? Why or why not?
- Does the weather change how you talk to other people?
- What kind of weather makes you want to stay home all day?

**Rain and Snow**

- Do you like rainy days? Why or why not?
- What's the best thing to do on a snowy day?
- Did you play in the snow when you were a child?
- Do you enjoy the sound of rain? Where do you like to listen?
- What do you wear on your feet when it rains?
- Have you ever been caught in the rain without an umbrella?
- Do you prefer snow or rain? Which is easier to live with?

**Getting Ready**

- Do you check the weather forecast every day? On which app?
- Do you trust the forecast? Has it ever been very wrong?
- How do you dress for a cold day?
- How do you stay comfortable in very hot weather?
- Do you keep an umbrella in your bag or at the office?
- What do you do to protect your skin from the sun?
- How many coats and jackets do you own for different weather?

**Weather and Plans**

- How does the weather affect your plans for the day?
- How does rain change what you do at the weekend?
- Have you ever canceled a trip because of the weather?
- Do you still go out in bad weather, or wait for a better day?
- What's the best weather for a family day trip outside the city?
- Does your work or school change when the weather is extreme?
- Has a special day ever been saved by a change in the weather?

**Seasons**

- What's your favorite season, and why?
- How many real seasons does your hometown have?
- What do you like to eat in summer, and what in winter?
- How does your wardrobe change from summer to winter?
- Which season is best for traveling in China, and why?
- What's something you look forward to every autumn?
- If you could keep only two seasons, which two would you choose?
