# Weather

**Preferences**

- What do you think of the weather in Beijing? Be honest.
- Which city in China do you think has the best weather, and why?
- Do you prefer hot or cold weather? Which is easier for you?
- What's your favorite season, and what makes it special for you?
- Would you rather vacation on a sunny beach or at a snowy mountain resort?
- What's your ideal weather for a lazy day off at home?
- Would you rather have four clear seasons, or mild weather all year?

**Daily Life**

- How does the weather change your mood on a normal workday?
- How does your routine change from summer to winter?
- Do you check the weather app every morning? What do you look for?
- What's your favorite thing to do on a rainy weekend?
- How do you dress for a day that starts cold and ends hot?
- Does bad weather keep you at home, or do you still go out?
- How do you plan a trip so bad weather does not ruin it?

**Seasons**

- Do you enjoy rainy days, or do they make you feel gloomy?
- What's the best thing about the first snow of winter?
- Do you enjoy winter sports like skiing or snowboarding?
- Have you ever built a snowman or had a snowball fight?
- Do you like the sound of rain? Where do you like to listen to it?
- Which season do you find the hardest to get through, and why?
- How do you feel when the seasons change and you catch a cold?

**Weather and Mood**

- Does sunshine make you feel happier? Give a recent example.
- How do gray, cloudy days affect your mood and your energy?
- Why do some people love storms and others fear them?
- Does the weather change how patient you are with other people?
- Do you find rain relaxing, or does it make you feel tired?
- What kind of weather makes you want to stay in bed all day?
- Has the weather ever pushed you to change a big decision?

**Extreme Weather**

- What's the most extreme weather you've ever been caught in? Tell us.
- Have your plans ever been ruined by unexpected weather? What happened?
- How do you prepare for a typhoon or a heavy rain warning?
- Have you been in a heatwave above 40°C? How did you cope?
- Do you worry that extreme weather is becoming more common?
- How does your city deal with heavy snow or flooding?
- Have you ever missed a train or a flight because of bad weather?

**Coping with Heat and Cold**

- How do you stay cool in summer without wasting electricity?
- How do you keep warm on a cold day, indoors and outdoors?
- Do you use the air conditioner all night, or just a fan?
- How do you protect your skin from strong summer sun?
- What do you eat and drink to feel better in hot weather?
- Do you get dry skin or allergies when the seasons change?
- How do you sleep well on very hot or freezing nights?
