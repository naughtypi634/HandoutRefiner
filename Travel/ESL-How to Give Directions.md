# Asking for Directions

1.  Am I close to the hospital?

2.  Am I heading in the right direction to ...?

3.  Am I on the right road to ... restaurant?

4.  Are we on the right road to Louis Hotel?

5.  Can I follow you?

6.  Can you give me directions to the nearest bus stop?

7.  Can you show me on the map?

8.  Can you show me which way to go using the map?

9.  Can you tell me how to get to the bookstore from here?

10. Can you tell me the best way of getting to...?

11. Could you help me, please? I'm looking for the bank.

12. Could you please guide me?

13. Could you please point me in the direction of the Times Square?

14. Could you tell me how to get to the bookstore?

15. Do you have a map?

16. Do you know how to get to the gallery? I've never been there before.

17. Do you know the train station?

18. Do you know where the closest post office is?

19. Does this bus go to ...?

20. Excuse me! Can you help me find Liam Primary School? This is my
    first time in the city.

21. Excuse me! Can you tell me the way to the museum?

22. Excuse me! Could you give me a few directions? I'm lost.

23. Excuse me! Could you tell me where the closest bank is?

24. Excuse me! Do you know where the church is?

25. Excuse me! How do I get to the post office?

26. Excuse me! I'm afraid I can't find a gas station. Do you know where
    I can find one?

27. Excuse me! I'm lost. Could you please help me find the 5th Avenue?

28. Excuse me! Is there a hospital near here?

29. Excuse me! Could you tell me the way to Fairfax Street?

30. Excuse me! Where is the airport?

31. How do I find the Thai restaurant?

32. How do I get to the bus stop?

33. I am looking for this address, am I in the right place?

34. I wonder if you could help me. I'm looking for the nearest bank.

35. In which direction is the nearest Gas Station?

36. Is there a sports shop around here?

37. Is there a supermarket near here?

38. Is this the right way to Brington Resort?

39. May I ask for some help? I need to get to ....

40. May I ask where the Marriott Hotel is?

41. May I ask you how to get to the Eaton Centre?

42. Pardon me! I'm lost. How do I get to the cafe?

43. Please tell me how to get to your apartment?

44. Sorry to bother you, but would you mind showing me the way to the
    post office?

45. Sorry to disturb you, but I'm lost. I'm looking for the Sydney Opera
    House.

46. What direction/road should I take?

47. What's the best way to the nearest supermarket?

48. What's the easiest way to get to the nearest cinema from here?

49. What's the quickest way of getting to the supermarket?

50. Where can I find the nearest bakery?

51. Where is the pharmacy?

52. Which road should I take to get to library?

53. Which way do I go to get to the beach?

54. Will you please tell me where the library is?

55. Would you show me the way to the clothes shop?

# Giving Directions

1.  16 Go straight ahead.

2.  After you pass a restaurant on your left, take a right at the
    crossroad.

3.  Continue past the primary school.

4.  Continue straight ahead for about a mile.

5.  Cross the junction and keep going for about 1 mile.

6.  Cross the taxi rank (Br.)/stand (Am.) and you are there.

7.  Drive to Paris Street and turn left.

8.  Go across the bridge.

9.  Go along the street.

10. Go as far as the roundabout (Br)/ traffic circle(Am).

11. Go down there.

12. Go past the cinema and you'll find the library.

13. Go straight ahead at the traffic lights.

14. Go straight and turn right after the gas station.

15. If you cross the street, you'll find a bookstore there!

16. It is behind the hospital.

17. It is between our office and the supermarket.

18. It is in front of the bus station.

19. It is in the centre of the town.

20. It'll be on your left.

21. It'll be on your right.

22. It'll be straight ahead of you.

23. It's near.

24. It's on the corner, across from the cafe.

25. It's opposite the bank.

26. It's that way.

27. It's the first turn on the right after the bank.

28. It's this way.

29. Keep going straight ahead; you will see it on the right.

30. Make a left turn when you see the bookshop.

31. Round the corner from the post office.

32. Take a left at the junction.

33. Take a left when you come to the main street.

34. Take a right at the junction.

35. Take the first left when you enter Steven Road.

36. Take the first left.

37. Take the second exit at the T- junction and then turn right at the
    traffic lights.

38. Take the second right.

39. Take this road.

40. The hospital is opposite the railway station.

41. The house is next to the ....

42. The shop is between the chemist/ pharmacy/drug store and KFC.

43. This straight road will lead you to the hospital.

44. Turn left at the end of the corridor.

45. Turn left at the intersection and the... is on the right.

46. Turn left.

47. Turn right at the crossroads.

48. Turn right at the end of the road and my house is number 67.

49. Turn right.

50. Walk past the park, and keep going straight until you see the sign
    for the museum.

51. When you get to Paris Street, take your next right.

52. You'll cross some railway lines.

53. You'll pass a museum on your left.

54. You're going in the wrong direction.

55. You're going the wrong way.

# How Far is It?

1.  How close is it?

2.  How far is it to the airport?

3.  How far is it to the park from here?

4.  How long does it take to get there?

5.  If there's no traffic, about 25 minutes. In heavy traffic, it takes
    about 45 minutes.

6.  Is it a long way?

7.  Is it far (from here)?

8.  It takes a while.

9.  It takes about 30 minutes.

10. It's a long way on foot.

11. It's a long way to walk.

12. It's about a five-minute walk.

13. It's about a mile from here.

14. It's about a twenty-minute bus ride.

15. It's just around the corner.

16. It's not far (from here).

17. It's not far, maybe about 200 yards.

18. It's pretty far from here.

19. It's quite a long way.

20. It's quite close.

# If You Can't Help

1.  I am a stranger here myself.

2.  I don't know the way. We've just moved in.

3.  I'm afraid I can't help you.

4.  I'm afraid I don't. I'd never been here before.

5.  I'm sorry! I'm not from here! I have no idea!

6.  I'm sorry, I don't know.

7.  I'm sorry. I'm not from here so I don't know my way around.

8.  Sorry, I'm not from around here.

9.  Sorry. You'll have to ask someone else.

10. You could ask the bus driver.

# Offer Warnings

1.  Go slow, there are many schools on the way.

2.  If you pass the museum, you went too far.

3.  It's a big hill. (if they are walking or on a bike)

4.  It's a one-way lane.

5.  It's a very busy road.

6.  It's very difficult to park downtown.

7.  Stay in the right lane.

8.  There might be construction.

9.  There might be road repairs.

10. There might still be road repairs and diversions.

11. There's no parking.
