# Toxic Behaviors

**Negative Talk**

- Consistent negativity — "Nothing ever goes right for me." Limit contact; steer talks to solutions.
- Blaming others — "It's not my fault; the team let me down." Ask calmly what they will do next.
- Constant criticism — "You call that a presentation? It was boring." Give one useful suggestion.
- Playing the victim — "Why does this always happen to me?" Point to one thing they control.

**Control and Manipulation**

- Manipulative behavior — "If you really cared, you would help me." Set clear boundaries, then hold them.
- Boundary pushing — "Why are you keeping this a secret?" State your limit once, then enforce it.
- Gaslighting — "That never happened; you remember it wrong." Keep notes and trust yourself.
- Passive-aggressive — "I'm fine," said with a sigh. Ask directly what is really wrong.

**Drama and Self-Absorption**

- Drama and chaos — "You won't believe what happened today!" Stay neutral; don't take sides.
- Self-centered — "Why should I help? What's in it for me?" Say clearly what you need.
- Jealousy and envy — "I can't believe you got that promotion." Share your wins without competing.
- Hot and cold — "I'll help" one day, gone the next. Write agreements down and follow up.

**Discussion**

- Which of these signs have you seen in a coworker or a friend?
- "I'm fine," said with a sigh — why is passive-aggression so common?
- How do you tell a bad day from a toxic pattern over time?
- What is a polite way to set a boundary with a relative?
- Someone gaslights you about a work meeting. What do you do next?
- Is it ever right to cut someone off completely? Give an example.
- How do you stay calm when someone twists what you said?
- Why do some people play the victim? What do they get from it?
- How do you handle constant criticism without starting a fight?
- Have you ever been the toxic one? What made you change?
- Is it better to confront a toxic person or quietly avoid them?
- What is one sentence you use to end a draining conversation?
- Do you keep a record when you disagree with someone at work?
- When should you stop trying to fix a difficult person?
