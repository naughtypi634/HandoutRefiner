# Business Discussion Questions

**Jobs & Career**

- What matters most in a job — money, enjoying your work, or what else?
- How do we find a good job? Apps, referrals, job fairs, or any other way?
- What helps people get a good job — a degree, real skills, or connections?
- How do people get a promotion — hard work, good relationships, or luck?
- What do you check first on a job ad — the salary, the city, or the hours?
- Is it OK to quit a job after only three months if you dislike it?
- What is a fair starting salary for a fresh graduate in your city?

**Company & Workplace**

- What company do you want to work for — a big one, a start-up, or another?
- What makes a meeting useful — a clear goal, a short time, or the right people?
- What helps a company react fast — small teams, good leaders, or what else?
- What makes a boss easy to work with? Give a real example from your life.
- Is it OK to send a work message at 11 p.m. on a Sunday? Why or why not?
- What does a company need to do to keep good people from leaving?
- Would you rather work in a quiet office or a noisy open-plan floor? Why?

**Work-Life Balance**

- How can people balance work and family — flexible hours, or what else?
- What is the best way to show you work hard — long hours or good ideas?
- How do you deal with work messages after work — reply, wait, or ignore?
- How many hours a week is too much? What would you do about it?
- Do you check your work phone on holiday? What does that say about your job?
- If your company pays for overtime, would you work every weekend?
- What does a real weekend look like for you — rest, family, or chores?

**Technology & the Future of Work**

- What will work look like — home offices, big offices, or something else?
- How will AI change work — help us, take our jobs, or both?
- Would you take a job where a robot or AI checks your daily work? Why?
- What new job do you think will exist in 2035 but not today?
- Is it rude to use AI to write an email to a colleague or a client?
- How do you keep your skills useful when everything changes so fast?

**Sales & Customers**

- How should companies sell — on WeChat, on live-streaming, or in shops?
- Who is a salesperson — the sales team, customer service, or everyone?
- What do good salespeople need — product knowledge, a friendly manner, or more?
- Have you ever talked a seller down on price? How did you do it?
- Would you pay 20% more for a product with a two-year warranty? Why?
- What makes you leave a shop or app without buying anything?
- Do you trust customer reviews more than a shop's own ads? Why?

**Business Ethics & Fairness**

- What should customers do about cheating companies — complain or stop buying?
- When is it OK for a company to pay less tax — never, or in hard times?
- How is work different for men and women — pay, promotions, or job types?
- Is it wrong to accept a gift from a client who wants a contract?
- Should a company tell staff before it plans to close a factory? Why?
- What should happen to a manager who takes money from the team?
