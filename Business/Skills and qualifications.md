# Education and training

- What was your major in school? What was the most important lesson you
  learned in your major?

- Do you think going to school helps you get a good job? Why?

- How does it translate into your current position, if at all?

- How important is paper qualification? Some people say it's the skills
  that matter the most while others think the paper itself is an
  indicator of skills. What do you think?

# Skills

- What are the skills that keep coming up in the JD part of a job AD?
  Why is that? Write down some. Among all the skills we need, which one
  is essential? What are your top 3 picks?

- What are the skills that you need for your job? Examples?

- What are your strong suits and what do you need to improve? Try to be
  specific.

- Can you name a skill you are proud of? How did you develop it?

- How do you prefer to learn new skills? (e.g., hands-on practice, 1 on
  1 tutoring, reading, watching videos)

- What are your hobbies? What did you get from your hobbies? Name one
  time when it came in handy.

# More discussion

- What's your take on people flocking to Master's of Business
  Administration (MBA)?

- Which of the following makes more sense to you? 2 yrs of actual
  working experience including internship or 2-3 yrs of further study
  for a master's degree overseas.

- Do you think all jobs need training? Why or why not?
