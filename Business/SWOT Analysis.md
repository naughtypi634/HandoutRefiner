# SWOT Analysis

## Personal SWOT Analysis

### Strengths

**Words**

- reliable — 靠得住的
- organized — 有条理的
- curious — 爱琢磨的
- patient — 有耐心的
- flexible — 能变通的
- detail-oriented — 注重细节的
- self-motivated — 不用人催的
- adaptable — 适应快的
- articulate — 说得清楚的
- focused — 专注的

**Phrases**

- breaking big tasks into small steps — 把大任务拆成小步
- picking up new software quickly — 新软件上手很快
- staying calm when a deadline is close — 截止日期近了也不慌
- explaining things in simple words — 能用简单的话讲清楚
- asking good questions in meetings — 会上问得出好问题
- keeping a steady sleep-and-work routine — 作息稳定
- saving a fixed amount every month — 每月固定存钱
- getting along with different kinds of people — 各种人都合得来
- using customer feedback to fix my work — 用客户反馈改自己的工作
- saying no when my plate is already full — 手头满了敢说不

**Sentence patterns**

- One thing I'm good at is ______. — 我擅长的一件事是……
- People often ask me to help with ______. — 大家常找我帮忙……
- I'm the person who ______. — 我是那个……的人
- I usually ______ before anyone asks. — 我通常会主动……
- My manager says I'm ______. — 我的主管说我……

- indecisive — 拿不定主意的

- careless — 粗心的

- nervous — 容易紧张的

**Phrases**

- running late in the morning — 早上总在赶时间
- keeping everything in my head — 什么都记在脑子里
- comparing myself with my classmates — 和同学比较
