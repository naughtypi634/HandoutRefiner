# Corporate Jargon

**A–F**

**Phrase**  **Meaning**  **中文**
----------  -----------  -------
Action plan  A step-by-step plan to reach a specific goal.  行动方案
Align  To get everyone to agree and work the same way.  保持一致
Back to the drawing board  To start a plan over from scratch.  推倒重来
Bandwidth  The time and energy to take on more work.  精力、余力
Bells and whistles  Extra features that are nice but not essential.  花哨的附加功能
Benchmark  A standard used to compare performance.  基准、标杆
Best practice  The most effective way to do something.  最佳实践
Blue-sky thinking  Creative ideas with no practical limits.  天马行空的想法
Boots on the ground  The people actually doing the work on site.  一线人手
Bottom line  The most important point or result.  关键结果
Brainstorm  To come up with many ideas quickly.  头脑风暴
Buy-in  Agreement or support for an idea.  认同、支持
Circle back  To come back to a topic later.  稍后再讨论
Core competencies  The main strengths a company is known for.  核心竞争力
Customer centric  Putting the customer's needs first.  以客户为中心
Deep dive  A close, detailed look at a topic.  深入剖析
Dot the i's and cross the t's  To check every small detail.  一丝不苟
Drill down  To look closely at the parts of a big number.  层层深挖
Drink the Kool-Aid  To believe in something without question.  盲目相信
Dumpster fire  A total disaster.  一团糟
Family/Rockstar/Legend  A word for an exceptionally talented colleague.  大神、顶梁柱

**G–L**

**Phrase**  **Meaning**  **中文**
----------  -----------  -------
Game-plan  A plan of action for reaching a goal.  行动方案
Get ducks in a row  To get everything organized first.  安排妥当
Get the ball rolling  To get something started.  开始启动
Going forward  From now on; in the future.  今后
Happy Monday  A cheerful hello sent to the team on a Monday.  周一问候
Have in back pocket  To keep a plan ready to use.  留有后手
Idea shower  A session where many ideas are thrown out.  点子会
In the driving seat  In control of what happens.  掌握主动
Joined-up thinking  Thinking that links different ideas or teams.  融会贯通
Let's table this  Let's set this topic aside for now.  先搁置
Level playing field  A situation where everyone has an equal chance.  公平的起跑线
Level set  To make sure everyone shares the same understanding.  对齐认知
Loop in  To include someone in a chat or email.  把某人拉进讨论
Low-hanging fruit  The easiest tasks or wins.  容易到手的成果

**M–R**

**Phrase**  **Meaning**  **中文**
----------  -----------  -------
Micromanager  A boss who checks every little thing you do.  事无巨细的老板
Moving the goalposts  Changing the rules to make a goal harder.  出尔反尔
No 'i' in team  A reminder that teamwork beats going solo.  团队里没有“我”
No brainer  A choice that is obviously easy or right.  显而易见的选择
On my radar  Something I am watching or aware of.  在我的关注范围内
On the same page  Sharing the same understanding.  想法一致
Open door policy  Managers welcome staff to come in and talk.  敞开大门
Out of the loop  Not being kept informed.  被排除在外
Pick up and run with it  To take an idea and carry it forward.  接手并推进
Pipeline  Work that is in progress or coming up.  在手、待办项目
Pivot  A change of direction or strategy.  转型
Play hardball  To be tough and aggressive in a deal.  来硬的
Pushback  Resistance or disagreement.  反对、抵制
Put a pin in it  To set a topic aside for later.  暂缓、先记下
Quick win  An easy task that gives a fast result.  速胜
Reinvent the wheel  To redo work that already exists.  重复造轮子
Results driven  Focused on getting results.  结果导向

**S–Z**

**Phrase**  **Meaning**  **中文**
----------  -----------  -------
Singing from the same hymn sheet  Saying the same thing as a group.  口径一致
Strategic fit  How well something matches the company's strategy.  战略契合度
Streamline  To make a process simpler and faster.  精简流程
Sync  To line up schedules or information.  同步
Takeaways  The key points to remember.  要点、收获
The idea's got legs  The idea is strong and likely to succeed.  站得住脚
Thinking outside the box  Thinking in a new, creative way.  跳出框框
Throw it up and see what sticks  To try many ideas and keep what works.  广撒网
To 'action' a project  To start carrying out a project.  着手执行
To 'park' a project  To pause a project without dropping it.  暂缓项目
To 'ping' an email  To send a quick email to check or remind.  发邮件提醒
To kick a project into the long grass  To delay a project with no date to return.  无限期搁置
To run an idea up the flagpole  To test an idea with senior people.  向高层试探
To take something offline  To discuss a topic privately, off the group.  会后私下聊
Touch base  To make quick contact with someone.  简单联络
Unpack  To break down and explain something in detail.  拆解讲清楚
Value added  Extra benefits that add worth.  增值
Window of opportunity  A short period when you can act.  时机窗口
Win-win  A deal that benefits everyone.  双赢
Work hard, play hard  To work long hours and enjoy life too.  拼命工作，尽情享受
