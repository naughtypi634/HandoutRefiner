\### Topics:

1\. \*\*Types of Photography\*\*: Explore different genres such as
portrait, landscape, street, wildlife, and macro photography.

2\. \*\*Famous Photographers\*\*: Discuss well-known photographers and
their styles, such as Ansel Adams, Dorothea Lange, or Annie Leibovitz.

3\. \*\*Photography Techniques\*\*: Talk about various techniques like
composition, lighting, and exposure.

4\. \*\*Cultural Significance of Photography\*\*: How photography
reflects culture and society in different parts of the world.

5\. \*\*Photography Equipment\*\*: Discuss different types of cameras,
lenses, and accessories.

6\. \*\*Editing and Post-Processing\*\*: Explore the role of software
like Adobe Photoshop or Lightroom in enhancing photos.

7\. \*\*Photography in Social Media\*\*: The impact of platforms like
Instagram on photography trends and styles.

8\. \*\*Personal Photography Projects\*\*: Encourage students to share
their own photography projects or ideas.

\### Questions:

1\. What inspired you to start taking photos?

2\. Do you prefer taking photos of people, nature, or objects? Why?

3\. What is your favorite photograph that you have taken? Can you
describe it?

4\. How do you choose the subjects for your photographs?

5\. What do you think makes a photograph great?

6\. Have you ever experimented with different photography styles? Which
ones did you enjoy?

7\. How do you feel about editing your photos? Do you think it changes
the original image?

8\. What role do you think photography plays in storytelling?

9\. Can you think of a photograph that had a significant impact on you?
What was it about?

10\. If you could take a photograph anywhere in the world, where would
it be and why?
