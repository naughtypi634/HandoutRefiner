# Why is body language important?

- We use it to judge other people's character. It is one of the first
  things we notice about a stranger.

- We use it to communicate when we cannot use words.

- Employers and interviewers often look at body language.

# List of Body Language

## Facial Expressions

- Avoiding eye contact = shy, worried, or lying

- A blank face (no expression) = hiding feelings or no emotion

- Direct eye contact = confidence

- Eyes looking into the distance = daydreaming, not focused

- Pressing lips together (tight lipped) = annoyed or angry

- Raised eyebrows = doubtful or surprised

- Smiling = friendly

## Physical Actions

- Hands behind the back and shoulders back = confident

- Arms crossed = defensive or insecure (sometimes angry)

- Bowing (bending at the waist) = greeting in some countries

- Biting nails = nervous

- Blushing (going red in the cheeks) = embarrassed

- Rubbing the eyes = tired or not believing something

- Covering an open mouth = scared or shocked

- Raising both hands with palms forward = showing submission or "stop"

- Scratching the head = confused

- Shaking the head = saying "no"

- Shrugging shoulders = "I don't know" or unsure

- Stroking the chin = thinking deeply

- Nodding = agreement or "yes"
