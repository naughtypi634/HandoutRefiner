# Art Discussion

**Art Vocabulary**

**Film Genres**

- action (noun): exciting, fast-paced.
- comedy (noun): funny, entertaining.
- drama (noun): serious, emotional.
- romance (noun): romantic, touching.
- horror (noun): scary, frightening.
- thriller (noun): suspenseful, tense.
- sci-fi (noun): creative, futuristic.
- fantasy (noun): magical, creative.
- animation (noun): colorful, imaginative.
- documentary (noun): informative, realistic.
- mystery (noun): mysterious, surprising.
- musical (noun): lively, entertaining.

**Music Genres**

- pop (noun): catchy, popular.
- rock (noun): powerful, energetic.
- hip-hop / rap (noun): rhythmic, expressive.
- R&B (noun): smooth, emotional.
- jazz (noun): creative, relaxing.
- classical music (noun): beautiful, calming.
- folk music (noun): traditional, simple.
- electronic music (noun): modern, energetic.
- country music (noun): relaxed, emotional.
- heavy metal (noun): loud, intense.

**Painting Vocabulary**

- portrait (noun): realistic, expressive.
- landscape (noun): peaceful, beautiful.
- still life (noun): detailed, colorful.
- abstract art (noun): unusual, creative.
- oil painting (noun): rich, vivid.
- watercolor (noun): light, delicate.
- sketch (noun): simple, rough.
- realistic painting (noun): lifelike, detailed.
- colorful painting (noun): bright, vivid.
- black-and-white painting (noun): dramatic, minimal.
- brushstroke (noun): bold, visible.
- composition (noun): balanced, interesting.

**Discussion Questions**

- What picture is on your phone wallpaper? What does it remind you of?

- What film scene made you laugh, cry, or think deeply? What happened in the scene?

- Have you ever bought something because you liked its design, such as a poster, phone case, notebook, or piece of clothing? What attracted you?

- What kind of art do you enjoy—music, films, paintings, dance, or something else? Why?

- Do you have art, photos, or posters at home? Where did you get them?

- Why do some people buy paintings or other art for millions of yuan? Is a high price a sign of high quality?

- Can a popular film be bad? Can an unpopular film be good? Give an example.

- Which themes appear again and again in art, such as love, family, loneliness, or freedom? Why do artists return to them?

- Do children create art more freely than adults? Why or why not?

- Do you have any hidden gems to share?

- In a fire, would you save a cat or the Mona Lisa? Why?

- Can we live without art?
