# Moral Dilemmas

**Money and Things**

- You find a wallet on the sidewalk with $500 in cash and the owner's ID inside. What do you do?
- Would you try to find the owner yourself, hand the wallet to the police, or leave it where you found it?
- You break an expensive vase at a friend's home. Do you tell them it was you?
- The cashier gives you 100 yuan too much in change. Do you give it back?
- What if it is only 5 yuan? Does the amount change your answer?

**Telling the Truth to Friends**

- Your best friend asks you to lie about where they were on Friday night. Do you do it?
- Their partner asks you straight out if you were together on Friday. What do you say?
- Your friend asks what you think of their new haircut, and it looks terrible. What do you tell them?
- You take one bite of a dish you cannot stand at a dinner party, and the host asks how it is. What do you say?
- You find out your friend's child is not biologically theirs. Do you tell them?

**Love, Friends and Boundaries**

- Your friend's ex asks you out on a date. Do you say yes?
- Would you talk to your friend about it first, or is it none of their business now?
- Your crush asks you to pretend to be their partner at a family dinner. Do you agree?
- Two of your friends are dating. One is planning to propose, and the other tells you they want to break up. What do you do?
- You know your younger brother plans to drop out of college. Do you tell your parents?

**Right and Wrong in Public**

- You see a stranger slip expensive jewelry into their bag in a store. What do you do?
- Do you report it, say something to them directly, or walk away?
- Two people behind you keep talking loudly in the movie theater. What do you say to them?
- Your neighbor's music is still blasting at 2 a.m. on a work night. What do you do?
- You are already late for an important meeting and see a stranger with a flat tire on the road. Do you stop?

**Work and Big Decisions**

- A colleague keeps asking you to help with their work, and you already have too much of your own. How do you say no?
- Do you tell them the truth, or say yes and do the work slowly?
- You overhear your coworkers planning a surprise party for you. Do you act surprised for them?
- You are offered your dream job in another city, but your partner does not want to move. Do you take it?
- Would you try long distance, or would you end the relationship over it?

**Small Awkward Moments**

- You notice food stuck in your date's teeth on a first date. Do you tell them?
- How would you say it without making them feel bad?
- Two close friends are getting married on the same day. How do you choose which wedding to go to?
- What decides it for you: the time of each wedding, or who invited you first?
