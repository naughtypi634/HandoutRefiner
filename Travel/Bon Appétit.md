**Warm up**

- What is your favorite type of cuisine, and why do you love it?

- Do you prefer dining in or ordering takeout? What's your go-to order?

- What's the best restaurant you've ever been to, and what made it
  special?

- Have you ever had a meal that completely changed your perspective on
  food? What was it?

- If you could only eat one type of food for the rest of your life, what
  would it be?

**Restaurant Experiences**

- What's the most memorable dining experience you've had?

- Have you ever had a bad experience at a restaurant? What happened?

- What's your favorite restaurant in your city, and what do you
  recommend there?

- Do you enjoy trying new restaurants, or do you prefer to stick to your
  favorites?

**Health**

- How do you balance enjoying food with maintaining a healthy diet?

- What's your opinion on organic or locally sourced foods?

- Do you have any dietary restrictions or preferences?

- How do you feel about meal prepping? Is it something you do?

- Have you heard of stress eating?

**Preferences/** **MISC. (miscellaneous)**

- What food do you find underrated or overrated?

- Are there any foods you absolutely refuse to eat? Why?

- How do you feel about plant-based or vegan diets?

- What's your opinion on food delivery services? Do you use them often?

- How important is the ambiance of a restaurant to your dining
  experience?

- What's your favorite meal of the day: breakfast, lunch, or dinner?
  Why?

- How do you feel about buffets? Do you enjoy them or find them
  overwhelming?

- What was your favorite food memory from childhood?

- Have you ever traveled somewhere specifically for the food? Where did
  you go?

- How do you balance enjoying food with maintaining a healthy diet?

- How do you feel about the use of technology in restaurants, like
  ordering via apps?
