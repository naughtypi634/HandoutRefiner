# Work-Life Balance

**Work and Time**

- 加班 — overtime：extra hours after your workday ends
- 通勤 — commute：the trip between home and your office
- 年假 — paid vacation days：days off that you still get paid for
- 弹性工时 — flexible hours：choosing when you start and stop
- 居家办公 — working from home：doing your job from your own place
- 大小周 — alternating weeks：five days one week, six the next
- 轮班 — shift work：working nights or weekends in turns
- 打卡 — clock in and out：recording when you start and leave
- 下班时间 — quitting time：the hour your workday officially ends
- 工作与生活平衡 — work-life balance：time for both your job and your life

**When Balance Breaks**

- 倦怠 — burnout：feeling empty and tired from months of stress
- 随时待命 — always on call：expected to answer at any hour
- 下班后消息 — after-hours messages：work texts that arrive at night
- 过劳 — overwork：working far more than your body can take
- 内卷 — rat race：a race to keep up that nobody can win
- 睡眠不足 — sleep debt：the sleep you keep missing
- 周末加班 — working weekends：losing your Saturday to the office
- 无法下线 — can't switch off：your mind stays at work after hours
- 工作焦虑 — work anxiety：worry about work that will not stop

**Setting Boundaries**

- 划清界限 — set boundaries：decide what work cannot take from you
- 下班断联 — unplug：stop checking work messages after hours
- 拒绝额外工作 — turn down extra work：say no to one more task
- 优先排序 — prioritize：decide what really comes first
- 委派 — delegate：hand part of the work to someone else
- 保护时间 — protect your time：keep some hours for yourself
- 准时下班 — leave on time：walk out when your hours are done
- 好好休息 — take a real break：rest with no phone and no laptop
- 静音通知 — mute notifications：stop the pings after work
- 预留缓冲 — block time：keep some empty hours in your day

**Warm-up Discussion**

- How many hours do you work in a normal week, overtime included?
- How much time do you have for yourself on a workday?
- How many vacation days do you get a year, and did you use them all last year?
- How long is your commute, and what do you do on the way?
- What helps you unwind after a long day at work?

**Discussion**

- Some companies work alternating weeks — five days one week, six the next. Could you work that schedule, and what would you ask for in return?
- When you work from home, when does your workday really end, and what did your last work-from-home day look like?
- Would you rather take one long vacation or several short ones, and why?
- Is your commute your own time or wasted time, and what would make it better?
- A coworker asks you to cover their weekend shift for the third time. How do you say no and keep the friendship?
- Your phone buzzes with work messages all weekend. What do you do to keep the weekend yours?
- If your job and your family both need you next Saturday, how do you decide?
- If you could design your perfect workday — start time, breaks, quitting time — what would it look like?
- Have smartphones and laptops made your work-life balance better or worse, and what is one example from your own week?
