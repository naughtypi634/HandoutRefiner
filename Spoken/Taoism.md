# Taoism

**Key Ideas**

- Wuwei 无为 — acting without forcing, and letting things flow.
- Yin and Yang 阴阳 — two opposite forces that balance each other.
- Dao 道 — the way; the natural order behind everything.
- De 德 — the quiet power you gain by following the Dao.
- Nature 自然 — the world as it is, not as we plan it to be.
- Simplicity 朴素 — wanting less, and living with less.
- Harmony 和谐 — moving with things, not against them.
- Inverse 反 — things often turn into their opposites.

**Famous Quotes**

- The journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step.
- Be simply yourself, stop comparing, and people will respect you.
- Nature does not hurry, yet everything is accomplished.
- Knowing others is intelligence; knowing yourself is true wisdom.
- Mastering others is strength; mastering yourself is true power.
- A good traveler has no fixed plans and is not intent on arriving.
- He who knows that enough is enough will always have enough.
- The best way to hold onto something is to let it go.
- The flame that burns twice as bright burns half as long.
- Happiness is the absence of the striving for happiness.

**Discussion**

- What do you know about Taoism? Where did you first hear about it?
- Which of these ideas do you already live by? Which would be the hardest?
- "Go with the flow" — when has it worked for you, and when did it backfire?
- Do you agree that wanting less brings more happiness? Give an example.
- Your phone buzzes with 200 work messages — how would wuwei handle that?
- Is there a time you forced a plan and regretted it? What happened?
- A friend keeps comparing salaries and holidays. How would a Taoist reply?
- Can simplicity survive in a city full of ads, deliveries and 24-hour shops?
- Which quote would you save as your phone wallpaper, and why?
- Should a leader control everything, or step back? Give a real example.
- Taoism says softness beats hardness, like water on rock. Do you agree?
- Has something you lost later turned out to be good? Tell us the story.
- Would you rather live fast and bright, or slow and steady? Why?
- Some people call Taoism lazy. How would you answer them?
- In ten years, what would a simpler life look like for you?
