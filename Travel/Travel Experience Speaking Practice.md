**Preparation**

Before you speak, write brief notes (not full sentences) on these key
points:

- Where? (country, city, places visited)

- When? (month, season, how long)

- Who? (alone, family, friends)

- What did you do? (3--4 main activities)

- How did you feel? (emotions about the trip)

Keep notes short and simple --- just keywords to guide you, not a
written script.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Speaking Framework**

**Opening (30 seconds)**

> Start with a clear introduction:

- \"I want to tell you about my trip to \*\*\[place\]\*\*.\"

- \"Last \*\*\[time\]\*\*, I visited \*\*\[place\]\*\* with
  \*\*\[who\]\*\*.\"

- \"I\'d like to share my experience traveling to \*\*\[place\]\*\*.\"

Then give 2--3 quick details: where, when, who, and how long.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Main Story (1--2 minutes)**

Describe 2--3 memorable experiences in order. Speak naturally, not too
fast:

Use these sentence starters:

- \"First, I \...\"

- \"Then, I \...\"

- \"After that, \...\"

- \"One day, \...\"

- \"The best part was when \...\"

- \"I remember \...\"

Add sensory details:

- \"It was \*\*\[hot/cold/crowded/quiet\]\*\*.\"

- \"I saw/heard/tasted \...\"

- \"The place looked like \...\"

- \"It smelled like \...\"

Include at least one personal reaction:

- \"I was surprised/excited/nervous because \...\"

- \"I couldn\'t believe \...\"

- \"I felt amazing when \...\"

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Closing (20--30 seconds)**

Wrap up with your overall feeling:

- \"It was an unforgettable experience.\"

- \"I learned so much about the culture.\"

- \"I would definitely go back.\"

- \"Overall, I really enjoyed it because \...\"

**Vocabulary**

  ----------------------------------------------------------------
  **Category**   **Words**
  -------------- -------------------------------------------------
  Adjectives     beautiful, crowded, friendly, historical, modern,
                 peaceful, challenging, interesting

  Verbs (past    visited, explored, discovered, tried, watched,
  tense)         attended, climbed, enjoyed, learned

  Time           last summer, two weeks ago, on the first day, in
  expressions    the morning, at night, finally

  Emotions       excited, surprised, nervous, amazed,
                 disappointed, grateful, grateful
